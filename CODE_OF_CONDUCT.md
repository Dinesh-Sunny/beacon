# Code of Conduct

Beacon exists for people going through hard moments. We hold this project to the same care.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to code or new to English.
- Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- Welcome people of every background, age, ability, faith and identity.
- Keep personal details out of issues and pull requests, yours and anyone else's.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, or jokes at someone's expense.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Talking down to people because of their circumstances, including people who claim support.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer privately through GitHub, [@Dinesh-Sunny](https://github.com/Dinesh-Sunny). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributors that break this code.
