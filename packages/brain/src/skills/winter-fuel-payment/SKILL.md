# Winter Fuel Payment

Hard rules (winter 2026 to 2027):
- Born on or before 27 June 1960.
- Lives in England, Wales or Northern Ireland. Scotland has its own winter payment.
- Not in a council-funded care home while getting Pension Credit, Universal Credit or ESA.

Verdicts:
- Born before 1960: likely, quoting the sentence with the cut-off date.
- Born in 1960 without a month: worth-checking, missing = their birth month.
- Lives in a care home: worth-checking.

Gotchas:
- Most people get it automatically. Do not tell them to apply unless the page says they need to.
- If their own income is over 35,000 a year, HMRC takes it back through tax. Beacon never asks that, so do not mention it unless the page sentence you quote is about it.
