# Free TV licence

Hard rules:
- Aged 75 or over.
- They or a partner living at the same address get Pension Credit.

Verdicts:
- 75 or over and gets Pension Credit: likely, quoting the sentence about being 75 or over and getting Pension Credit.
- 75 or over, no Pension Credit yet, but Pension Credit is open for them: worth-checking, and say in why that claiming Pension Credit can bring this too.
- 75 or over, income well-over-line and no Pension Credit: unlikely-right-now.

Gotchas:
- Registered blind gives 50% off at any age. Mention it only if the page says so and they said it applies.
- They apply to TV Licensing, not DWP.
