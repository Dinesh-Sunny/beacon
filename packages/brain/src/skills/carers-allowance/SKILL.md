# Carer's Allowance

Hard rules:
- Aged 16 or over.
- Cares for someone at least 35 hours a week.
- The person they care for gets Attendance Allowance, PIP daily living, DLA middle or higher care (or the Scottish versions).
- Earnings 204 a week or less after tax and expenses (about 884 a month).
- Not in full-time study (21 hours or more a week).
- Scotland has Carer Support Payment instead.

Verdicts:
- Cares 35+ hours, cared-for person gets a qualifying benefit, earnings none, up-to-408 or 409-884: likely, quoting the 35 hours sentence or the benefit list sentence.
- Cared-for benefit not sure: worth-checking, missing = which benefit the person they care for gets.
- Earnings 885-1083 or over-1083: unlikely-right-now.
- Earnings rather-not-say: worth-checking.

Gotchas:
- No savings test: never mention savings.
- If they get a State Pension of about 86.45 a week or more they may not be paid, but have "underlying entitlement" that can raise Pension Credit. Use worth-checking for people over State Pension age and say why.
- Claiming can reduce the cared-for person's own benefits (severe disability addition). Mention it in why only if the page says so.
