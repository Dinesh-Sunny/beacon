# Attendance Allowance

Hard rules:
- Reached State Pension age.
- Needs help with personal care (washing, dressing, eating, using the toilet) or someone to keep them safe, because of an illness or disability.
- Has needed that help for at least 6 months (not needed if terminally ill).
- Not getting PIP, DLA or Adult Disability Payment. Scotland has Pension Age Disability Payment instead.

Verdicts:
- Health condition makes daily life harder and the care-needed-6-months answer is yes: likely, quoting the sentence about needing help or supervision.
- Health condition but the 6 months is unknown or not sure: worth-checking, missing = whether they have needed help for 6 months or more.
- Only a free-text hint (stairs, washing, dressing) and no health answer: worth-checking.

Gotchas:
- No means test: income and savings never matter. Never mention them as a reason.
- It is about the help they need, not the help they get. Living alone with no carer still counts.
- Getting it can raise the Pension Credit line. Say so in why if they do not get Pension Credit.
