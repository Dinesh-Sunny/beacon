# Council Tax Reduction

Hard rules:
- They must pay (be liable for) council tax on their main home. Living with family or in a care home usually means someone else pays.
- Each council runs its own scheme. The GOV.UK page sends people to their council, so the page rarely settles the amount.

Pension age (national rules):
- Savings under 16,000 is a hard cap, ignored if they get Guarantee Credit.
- Gets Guarantee Credit: likely (full reduction is common), quoting the page line about getting help if you get Pension Credit or are on a low income.

Working age (local schemes, Brent as the example):
- Savings cap can be 6,000, not 16,000. 6000-15999 at working age is worth-checking, not likely.
- Income counted excludes most DWP and HMRC benefits. Weekly earnings over about 250 (about 1,083 a month) usually means no reduction in Brent.
- Gets Universal Credit: in some councils the UC notice counts as a claim. Still worth-checking, as they may need to apply.

Gotchas:
- Other adults at home reduce it. Living alone gives a separate 25% single person discount - mention it in why only if the page mentions it.
- Without a council named, prefer worth-checking with missing set to which council they pay.
