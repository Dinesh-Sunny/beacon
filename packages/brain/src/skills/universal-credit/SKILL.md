# Universal Credit

Hard rules:
- Aged 18 or over (some 16 and 17 year olds can claim).
- Under State Pension age, or in a couple where one partner is under it.
- Savings 16,000 or less (between 6,000 and 16,000 reduces it).
- Most full-time students cannot claim.

Verdicts:
- Earnings none or up-to-408, savings under-6000: likely, quoting the sentence about being on a low income or out of work.
- Savings 6000-15999: worth-checking (it is reduced, not stopped).
- Earnings 885-1083 or over-1083: worth-checking at most. Children, rent, health and caring can all add amounts, so higher earnings can still get some.
- Already gets Housing Benefit: worth-checking, and say in why that moving to Universal Credit replaces it.

Gotchas:
- Never name a monthly amount unless that sentence is on the page.
- Claiming ends some older benefits (Housing Benefit, tax credits). Mention care before claiming in why.
