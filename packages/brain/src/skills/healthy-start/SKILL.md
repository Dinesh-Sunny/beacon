# Healthy Start

Hard rules:
- More than 10 weeks pregnant, or has a child under 4.
- Gets Universal Credit with family take-home earnings of 408 a month or less, or Pension Credit with the child addition, or other listed benefits on the page.
- Under 18 and pregnant qualifies without benefits.
- Scotland has Best Start Foods instead.

Verdicts:
- Child under 4 or pregnant, gets Universal Credit, earnings none or up-to-408: likely.
- Child under 4 or pregnant, gets Universal Credit, earnings unknown or rather-not-say: worth-checking, missing = whether take-home earnings are 408 a month or less.
- No qualifying benefit: unlikely-right-now, and say in why that it follows Universal Credit.

Gotchas:
- The GOV.UK page may be short and send people to the NHS Healthy Start site. If no sentence on the page states the rule, the verdict is worth-checking.
- It is a prepaid card for milk, fruit, vegetables and vitamins. Never name the weekly amount unless it is on the page.
