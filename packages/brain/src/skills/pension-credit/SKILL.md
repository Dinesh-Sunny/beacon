# Pension Credit

Hard rules (a clear fail means unlikely-right-now):
- Reached State Pension age. Couples: both must have reached it, unless already getting Pension Credit or pensioner Housing Benefit. A mixed-age couple claims Universal Credit instead.
- Lives in England, Scotland or Wales. Northern Ireland runs its own scheme.

Income line (Guarantee Credit): about 238 a week single, 363.25 a week couple. The person gives a band against that line, never an amount.
- under-line: likely, if the hard rules hold. Quote the sentence that sets the weekly amount it tops up to.
- around-line: worth-checking. Extra amounts (disability, caring, housing costs) raise the line, so people just over it can still get it.
- well-over-line: usually unlikely-right-now, but worth-checking if they have a disability benefit, care for someone or pay rent, because those raise the line.
- rather-not-say: worth-checking.

Savings: there is no upper limit. Savings over 10,000 only count as some income (tariff), so never rule it out on savings alone.

Gotchas:
- It opens the door to Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction, Warm Home Discount, a free TV licence at 75 and more. Say so in why when the verdict is likely or worth-checking.
- Never name a weekly amount unless that sentence is on the page.
