# Housing Benefit

Hard rules:
- They rent. A mortgage or owning outright never counts (that is Support for Mortgage Interest, not this).
- Single and reached State Pension age, or both partners have. Any age in supported or temporary housing or a refuge.
- Not getting Universal Credit (unless in supported or temporary housing).
- Not living in a close relative's home as their tenant.

Savings: over 16,000 means no Housing Benefit, unless they get Guarantee Credit, which ignores savings.

Verdicts:
- Gets Guarantee Credit and rents: likely, quoting the line about help if you get Pension Credit or are on a low income.
- Over State Pension age, rents, income under-line or around-line, savings under 16,000: likely or worth-checking (worth-checking when the landlord answer is unknown).
- Landlord is a close relative: unlikely-right-now.
- Income well-over-line: worth-checking at most - rent level matters and Beacon never asks the rent.

Gotchas:
- Never name an amount: it depends on rent, which Beacon never asks.
- A mixed-age couple cannot make a new claim: they get help with rent through Universal Credit.
- No settled home: open at any age if they are in temporary accommodation (such as a council B&B), a refuge, or supported or sheltered housing. Use worth-checking, missing = the kind of place they stay in.
