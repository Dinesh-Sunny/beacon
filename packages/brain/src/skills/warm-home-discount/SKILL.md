# Warm Home Discount

Hard rules (England and Wales):
- On the qualifying date (23 August 2026) they or their partner got Pension Credit (Guarantee Credit), Universal Credit, Housing Benefit or income-related ESA.
- Their own or their partner's name is on the electricity bill.
- Northern Ireland is not covered. Scotland has its own route that may need an application.

Verdicts:
- Gets one of the qualifying benefits and the electricity bill is in their name: likely, quoting the line that lists who qualifies.
- Gets a qualifying benefit but the bill name is unknown: worth-checking, missing = whether the electricity bill is in their name or their partner's.
- Does not get a qualifying benefit now but may get Pension Credit: worth-checking, and say in why that claiming Pension Credit can bring this too.
- Bill not in their name (for example they live with family): unlikely-right-now.

Gotchas:
- It is usually paid automatically. Do not tell them to apply unless the page says so.
- Their supplier must be in the scheme. Most large suppliers are.
