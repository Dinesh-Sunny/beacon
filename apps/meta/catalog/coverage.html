5 · Connectors

Where the data comes from

SourceKindAccessWhat it gives Beacon
GOV.UK Content APIapiopenThe structured JSON behind any GOV.UK page: GET https://www.gov.uk/api/content/<path>. Gives body text, parts, updated dates and links for every central scheme page.
GOV.UK Search APIsearchopenFinds GOV.UK pages by filters such as content_store_document_type, mainstream_browse_pages and organisations, with aggregates. Use it to list every benefit, grant and 'answer' page to diff against the packs.
GOV.UK benefits calculators pagepageopenNames the government-endorsed independent calculators (entitledto, Turn2us, Policy in Practice) and the groups they do not serve well.
entitledtopagelicenceComparator: public benefits calculator and a paid adviser calculator. Use to sanity-check Beacon results by hand, not as a data source.
Policy in Practice Better Off CalculatorpagelicenceComparator: Better Off Calculator, plus LIFT analytics that councils license to find residents missing support.
Turn2us benefits calculatorpagelicenceComparator: benefits calculator that organisations can embed.
mygov.scotpagescrapeOfficial pages for Social Security Scotland benefits and the Scottish Welfare Fund.
gov.walespagescrapeWelsh Government schemes: DAF, School Essentials Grant, EMA Wales, Nest and Council Tax Reduction Scheme.
nidirectpagescrapeNorthern Ireland benefits, Discretionary Support, rate relief and free school meals.
Scottish Welfare Fund via councilspageopenSWF is national policy but applied through each of Scotland's councils, so link the person to their council's SWF page using the council lookup.
GOV.UK local-authority APIapiopenPostcode to council name, homepage URL, tier and slug. Answered on 2026-11-04 with {name: 'London Borough of Brent', tier: 'unitary', slug: 'brent'}.
GOV.UK local transactions (Local Links Manager via Content API)apiopenGOV.UK 'local transaction' pages carry an LGSL service code; GOV.UK maps each code to the right page on every council's website. This is the fastest way to get a per-council link for services like Council Tax Reduction, Blue Badge and bus passes.
postcodes.ioapiopenPostcode to admin_district and its ONS code (for example Brent, E09000005), country, ward, parish, LSOA and deprivation index. Already used by Beacon for nation.
ONS Open Geography PortaldatasetopenOfficial names and codes lists for every local authority district and county in the UK, and lookups between them. The master list of councils for 'councils x local schemes'.
Council websitespagescrapeEach council's own pages for crisis help, DHP, discounts and local grants.
Policy in Practice LIFTdatasetlicenceCouncil-licensed analytics that link council data to find residents missing support. A possible partner route, not a source.
Ofcom social tariffs listpagescrapeEvery broadband and mobile social tariff: provider, tariff, monthly price and which benefits count (34 tariffs from 28 providers on 2026-11-04).
CCW water company list and help pagespagescrapeThe 20 water and sewerage companies (plus 11 NAV companies) and CCW's own guidance on social tariffs and WaterSure.
Ofwat customer assistancepagescrapeRegulator overview of WaterSure, special assistance and charitable trusts run by water companies.
Ofgem consumer pagespagescrapePSR, ECO and energy debt guidance; the regulator view of supplier obligations.
GOV.UK Content API (education pages)apiopenEngland's school meals, transport, bursary and childcare pages.
Get Information about SchoolsdatasetopenRegister of schools in England with their local authority; useful to point to the right council's school transport and uniform pages.
Council school pagespagescrapeUniform grants, school clothing grants (Scotland) and local free school meal top-ups set by each council.
nhs.uk pagespagescrapeHelp with health costs in England: travel, wigs, certificates, continuing healthcare.
NHSBSA help with health costspagescrapeExemption certificates, PPCs and the Low Income Scheme forms.
360Giving GrantNavdatasetopenGrants to organisations, for the phase 2 'groups' and 'firms' people.
360Giving API and DatastoreapiopenProgrammatic access to the same grants data, refreshed nightly; the Datastore offers read-only database logins for large one-off pulls.
Charity Commission register APIapikeyCharity details for England and Wales: objects, area of operation, contacts, classification (for example 'relief of poverty', 'grants to individuals').
Charity Commission full register downloaddatasetopenDaily extract of the whole England and Wales register in JSON and tab-delimited files (13 tables, including classification and area of operation).
OSCR Scottish Charity Register downloaddatasetopenDaily download of Scotland's 24,982 charities with names, addresses and purposes.
Charity Commission for Northern Ireland registersearchscrapeNorthern Ireland charity register.
Find that CharityapiopenOne record per charity across UK regulators; add .json to any charity URL; OpenRefine reconcile endpoint.
Turn2us Grants SearchsearchlicenceThe best curated list of grants for individuals (over 1,700 organisations), with eligibility filters.
GOV.UK Content API (employment pages)apiopenStatutory pay, redundancy and insolvency rights pages.
AcaspagescrapePlain-English employment rights guidance and helpline.
MoneyHelperpagescrapeGovernment-backed money guidance (Money and Pensions Service): debt advice locator, Pension Wise, a pilot benefits calculator powered by Inbest.
FCA consumer pages and Financial Services RegisterpagescrapeRules firms must follow (basic bank accounts, Consumer Duty) and which firms are authorised.
Find pension contact details (Pension Tracing Service)searchopenProvider contact details from an employer or provider name; also by phone on 0800 731 0175.
HMRC find a Child Trust FundsearchopenHMRC tells the person where the fund was opened, usually by letter within 3 weeks.
My Lost AccountsearchopenFree tracing of bank, building society and NS&I accounts.
Reclaim FundpageopenRuns the UK Dormant Assets Scheme (banks since 2011, insurance and pensions since 2023, investments since 2025). Explains that reclaims go through the original firm.
Unclaimed estates listdatasetopenThe Government Legal Department's list of estates waiting for relatives, updated several times a week in 2026.
NS&I prize checkersearchopenPremium Bonds prize check by holder's number.
GOV.UK Content API (justice pages)apiopenHelp with Fees, legal aid and criminal injuries pages.
Ombudsman websitespagescrapeHow and when to complain to each ombudsman.
