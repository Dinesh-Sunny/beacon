# Search the Armed Forces Memorial roll of honour

Source: https://www.gov.uk/search-armed-forces-memorial-roll-of-honour
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2014-12-10T13:52:22+00:00

Find out if someone's listed on the Armed Forces Memorial roll of honour - information available, print a certificate

You can search the Armed Forces Memorial roll of honour to see if someone’s listed on it.

 The Memorial commemorates members of the armed forces who died while in service either:

 on or after 1 January 1948

 in Palestine between 1945 and 1947

 Entries on the roll of honour database include:

 rank

 type of service

 regiment or corps

 date of death

 burial place, if known

 whether someone’s name is on the Memorial in the National Memorial Arboretum 

 No information is available until one year after someone’s death.

 You can download or print a certificate that will contain details of their service in the Armed Forces from the roll of honour.

You need to know the person’s last name or service number.

 If you know their first name or initial, you can use this to find the correct entry more quickly.

 If you have a question about the Armed Forces Memorial roll of honour, contact the Joint Casualty and Compassionate Centre ( JCCC ).
