# Find a Disabled Students' Allowance assessment provider

Source: https://www.gov.uk/disabled-students-allowances-assessment-centre
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2020-03-09T12:31:36+00:00

Find your nearest Disabled Students' Allowance assessment provider.

Enter your postcode to find your Disabled Students’ Allowance ( DSA ) assessment provider.

 This page is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 Only book an assessment if you’ve been told you need one by:

 Student Finance England

 Student Finance Wales

 If you live in Northern Ireland, contact your local student finance office .

 The assessment fee will be covered by your DSA . You do not have to pay for the assessment yourself.
