# Find out about Right to Buy from your council

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-out-about-right-to-buy-from-your-council
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-03-23T17:19:21+00:00

Get information from your local council about the right to buy your council house or flat, including where to send the application form

You can apply to your council for the right to buy your council home. Your council may also be able to help you complete the application.

Available in England only.

 There are different rules for Wales , Scotland and Northern Ireland.
