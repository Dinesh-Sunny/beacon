# Find a job in teaching or education

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-teaching-job
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2018-11-29T09:19:55+00:00

Search for a job in teaching or education in England, including jobs in leadership, education support and special educational needs and disabilities (SEND).

Search for jobs in state-funded schools and trusts in England.

 You can search for jobs in:

 teaching

 school leadership – for example, headteacher or head of department

 education support – for example, teaching assistant or learning cover supervisor

 special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) – for example, a SEND coordinator

 For jobs outside England:

 search for jobs in Scotland 

 search for jobs in Wales 

 search for jobs in Northern Ireland
