# Apply for short-term care for your child

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-short-term-care-for-child
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-12-23T10:53:16+00:00

Apply through your local council for short-term ('respite') care for your sick or disabled child to enable you to have a break from caring 

Apply for a short break from caring for a disabled child, also known as ‘respite care’. Your local council will tell you what clubs and activities are available for children with disabilities in your area.

Available in England and Wales only
