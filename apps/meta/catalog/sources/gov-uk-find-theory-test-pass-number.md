# Find your lost theory test pass certificate number

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-theory-test-pass-number
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-08-15T16:10:28+01:00

Find your lost theory test pass certificate number if you need it to book your driving test

What you need to do depends on whether you want the number to:

 book your test

 take to your test

 If you’re booking your test

 You usually do not need your theory test pass certificate number to book your driving test. 

 If DVSA asks for your theory test pass certificate number, you can request it.

 If you need to request your theory test pass certificate number

 You can request your theory test pass certificate number online . 

 You’ll need:

 your UK driving licence

 your booking reference number - this was sent to the email address you used to book your theory test

 If you cannot find your booking reference number, you can ask for your booking details in the service.

 Your theory test pass certificate number will be sent to the email address you used to book your theory test.

 If you’re taking your test

 You may need another way to prove that you’ve passed your theory test if you’ve lost your certificate. What you need to do depends on the type of test.

 If you’re taking a car driving test

 You do not need to get a replacement theory test certificate to take to your car driving test.

 Your driving examiner will check that you’ve passed your theory test before your driving test starts.

 If you’re taking any other type of driving test

 Contact DVSA with your:

 full name as it appears on your driving licence

 address

 driving licence number

 date of birth

 You’ll be sent a letter that you can take to your test instead of your pass certificate.
