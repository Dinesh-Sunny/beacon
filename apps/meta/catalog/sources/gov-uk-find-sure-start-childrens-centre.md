# Find a Sure Start Children's Centre

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-sure-start-childrens-centre
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-06-29T16:01:07+01:00

Search for Sure Start Children's Centres near to your home to find out about daycare and parenting advice

Sure Start centres give help and advice on child and family health, parenting, money, training and employment.

 Some centres also provide early learning and full day care for pre-school children.

 Contact your local council to find out about Sure Start centres in England.

 There are different services in Northern Ireland , Scotland and Wales .
