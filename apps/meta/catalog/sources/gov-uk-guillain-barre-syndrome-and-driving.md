# Guillain Barré syndrome and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/guillain-barre-syndrome-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-06T14:22:18+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have Guillain Barré syndrome - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if you have Guillain Barré syndrome.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form CN1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form CN1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
