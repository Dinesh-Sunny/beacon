# Estimate your Income Tax for a previous tax year

Source: https://www.gov.uk/estimate-income-tax-previous-year
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-11-22T14:46:53+00:00

Use HM Revenue and Customs’ (HMRC) tax checker to get an estimate of how much Income Tax you should have paid in a previous tax year.


Use this service to estimate how much Income Tax you should have paid for a previous tax year.

 You need to be:

 a basic or higher rate taxpayer earning under £100,000

 getting the Personal Allowance

 There are different ways to:

 see how much Income Tax you paid last tax year (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026)

 check your Income Tax payments for the current tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027)

 estimate your Self Assessment tax bill for the current tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027)

 You may be able to claim a refund if you’ve paid too much.

 This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

You need details of:

 your earnings, before and after tax - get this from your P60 

 any savings - get this from your bank statements or annual statement from your bank or building society

 any Gift Aid donations you’ve made to charity

 Who cannot get an estimate online

 You cannot get an estimate online if you:

 earn £100,000 or more

 get taxable state benefits 

 are entitled to claim Married Couple’s Allowance 

 have other taxable income, for example from dividends and trusts

 are a higher rate taxpayer and want to estimate your Gift Aid tax relief

 get the Winter Fuel Payment (or Pension Age Winter Heating Payment in Scotland) and your total income is over £35,000

 You can check you’ve paid the right tax by contacting HMRC or by getting help from an accountant .
