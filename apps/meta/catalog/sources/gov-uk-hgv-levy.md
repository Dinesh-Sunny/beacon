# Pay the heavy goods vehicle (HGV) levy

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hgv-levy
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-06-02T17:46:03+01:00

Register and pay for the HGV levy

You need to pay the HGV levy before entering the UK if the HGV :

 is not registered in the UK

 weighs 12,000kg or more

 is not exempt

 Sign in or create an account

 You do not have to sign up for an account to use this service, but if you do you can:

 pay the HGV levy faster

 change the dates you want the levy to start (up until 1 day before)

 get a refund

 pay for a levy for more than 1 month

 see your payment history

 register and manage your vehicles

 allow someone else to manage your account

 You can also check if you’ve paid a levy on your HGV . You’ll need to know the front licence plate number of the vehicle you want to check.

 How to pay

 You can pay by:

 credit or debit card

 fuel card

 prepayment through your account

 bank transfer

 PayPal

You can also pay the HGV levy by phone.

 HGV levy call centre 
Telephone: +44 33 0101 3725 
Every day, 6am to 6pm 
 Find out about call charges 

 Exemptions for zero emission HGVs

 You do not need to pay the HGV levy if your vehicle is zero emission.

 You can choose to register your vehicle with the Department for Transport to help the system recognise it is zero emission. This reduces the chance of you getting stopped for not paying.

 Find out how to register a zero emission HGV in the exemptions section of the HGV levy service guidance .
