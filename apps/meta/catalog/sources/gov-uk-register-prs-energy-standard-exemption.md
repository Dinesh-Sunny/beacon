# Register a private rented sector energy standards exemption

Source: https://www.gov.uk/register-prs-energy-standard-exemption
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2026-05-05T10:10:03+01:00

Register or manage a private rented sector energy standard exemption: what you’ll need, sign in, how to get help.

Use this service to register an exemption for a private rented sector property that does not meet the Minimum Energy Efficiency Standards (MEES).

 Find out more about the standards if you are a landlord of either:

 a ‘domestic’ (residential) property 

 a ‘non-domestic’ (commercial, industrial or public) property 

 You can also use this service to manage existing exemptions.

 Before you start

 If you are registering a new exemption, you’ll need:

 your details, or the landlord’s details if you are an agent acting on their behalf

 the address of the property

 a valid energy performance certificate (EPC)

 evidence of how your property qualifies, depending on the exemption you are registering

 Find out more about the types of exemption you can register and the evidence needed .

 Register or manage an exemption

 You’ll need to sign in with GOV.UK One Login to use this service. You’ll be able to create a GOV.UK One Login if you do not already have one.

 The way to sign in to this service changed on 5 May 2026.

 Start now 

 After you register a new exemption

 You can rent your property immediately after registering a valid exemption. Your local council may contact you to ask for more information.

 If you need help
