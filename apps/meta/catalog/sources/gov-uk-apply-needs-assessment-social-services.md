# Apply for a needs assessment by social services

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-needs-assessment-social-services
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-01-16T15:16:32+00:00

Apply for a needs assessment by social services through your local council - support, home care help, disability equipment, day centres, care homes

A health and social care assessment is carried out by social services to find out what help and support you need - like healthcare, equipment, help in your home or residential care.

Services can include:

 home care help with things like cleaning and shopping

 disability equipment and adaptations to your home

 day centres to give you or the person who cares for you a break

 day care for your child

 help with parenting, such as parenting classes

 care homes

 Your local NHS service, Citizens Advice Bureau or a charity might be able to help you apply for a needs assessment.

Available in England and Wales only
