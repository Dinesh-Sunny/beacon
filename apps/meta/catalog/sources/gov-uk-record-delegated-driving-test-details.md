# Record delegated driving test details

Source: https://www.gov.uk/record-delegated-driving-test-details
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2022-11-16T12:00:00+00:00

Record the details of scheduled delegated driving tests with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA)

Record the details of scheduled delegated driving tests. You must be a registered delegated driving examiner or administrator authorised by the delegated examiner.

Tests you must record

 You must record the details of the following tests:

 licence acquisition tests

 Driver Certificate of Professional Competence ( CPC ) practical tests

 Read the Guide to delegated driving examiner test notifications .

 Paying for Driver CPC tests

 You have to pay to record Driver CPC test details. You can pay in 2 ways:

 by debit or credit card

 by debit from a pre-funded account

 Setting up a pre-funded account lets you deposit funds with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ) in advance and pay the fee by debiting the amount directly from your account.
