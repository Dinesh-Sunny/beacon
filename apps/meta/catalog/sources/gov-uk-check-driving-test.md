# Check your driving test appointment details

Source: https://www.gov.uk/check-driving-test
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-03-14T17:36:38+00:00

Check the date and time of your driving test appointment if you've lost or deleted your driving test confirmation email.

Use this service to check your driving test appointment details. You can check the:

 date and time of your driving test

 address of the driving test centre

 email address and phone number on your booking

 You must have your own contact details on your booking. You cannot use someone else’s details, for example your driving instructor’s. You can change the email address and phone number on your booking by viewing the booking and selecting ‘change’ next to ‘candidate details’.

 You do not need to take your booking confirmation to your driving test.

 This service is available from 6am to 11:40pm.

You need your:

 UK driving licence number

 theory test pass certificate number (from the letter you got when you passed your theory test) or your driving test reference number if you have it

 You can find your theory test pass certificate number if you’ve lost your letter.
