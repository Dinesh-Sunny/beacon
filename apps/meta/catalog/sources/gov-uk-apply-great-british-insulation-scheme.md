# The Great British Insulation Scheme

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-great-british-insulation-scheme
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-10-28T15:28:29+00:00

Information for eligible applicants about free or cheaper insulation to reduce your home’s energy bills - including how to make a complaint about installation.

The service to check if you’re eligible for free or cheaper home insulation has closed. Some energy suppliers are however still accepting applications. Check with your supplier.

 The Great British Insulation Scheme helps people get free or cheaper insulation to reduce their home’s energy bills.

 If you’ve already applied

 If you’re eligible for support, your energy supplier will arrange an assessment of your property.

 You’ll find out after the assessment if you need to pay anything towards the installation of the insulation.

 You can decide to not go ahead if you do not agree with assessment or costs.

 Installations must be complete by 31 March 2026, when the scheme ends.

 If you have questions about your existing application, contact the energy supplier you applied to.

 Make a complaint

 To complain about insulation installed under the scheme, follow the Ofgem energy company obligation complaints guidance .

 Read more about the Great British Insulation Scheme .
