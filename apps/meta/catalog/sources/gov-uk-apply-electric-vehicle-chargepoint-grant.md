# Apply for an electric vehicle chargepoint grant for your workplace

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-electric-vehicle-chargepoint-grant
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2018-07-25T11:40:35+01:00

Apply for a Workplace Charging Scheme grant to help pay for installing electric vehicle (EV) chargepoints at your workplace.


Use this service to apply for help with the cost of buying and installing electric vehicle ( EV ) chargepoints at your workplace.

 You can get up to 75% of the total cost up to a maximum of:

 £350 for each socket

 40 sockets across all sites – for example, if you’re installing chargepoint sockets at 40 sites, you can get help to pay for 1 socket at each site

 There is a different grant for state-funded education institutions .

 If you want to install an EV chargepoint at your home, find out about an electric vehicle chargepoint grant for renters or flat owners .

 Eligibility

 You can usually apply if you run a:

 business, including a small accommodation business

 charity

 public sector organisation

 Your organisation must be based in England, Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland.

 Apply for a grant

 You’ll need your organisation’s company registration number ( CRN ) or VAT identification number.
