# Rent a council garage

Source: https://www.gov.uk/rent-council-garage
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:20+01:00

Apply to rent a garage from the local council - there may be a waiting list

You can apply to rent a garage from the local council - there may be a waiting list for council garages.

You must be over 18

 Available in England and Wales only
