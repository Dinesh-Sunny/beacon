# Warm Homes Loan Scheme

Source: https://www.gov.uk/warm-homes-loan-scheme
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2026-10-28T00:01:08+00:00

## Overview

A Warm Homes loan could help you pay for energy saving improvements to your home, such as heat pumps or solar panels.

 The loan is provided by a lender (for example, a bank).

 After the improvement work is done, it might be checked as part of an audit .

 If you’re on a low income

 If you’re on a low income, you might be able to get free energy saving improvements made to your home instead through the Warm Homes: Local Grant or other grants .

 Using the Warm Homes Loan Scheme with other funding

 You may be able to get a Warm Homes loan as well as other grants or money from other funding schemes, including:

 the Boiler Upgrade Scheme 

 the Home Energy Scotland grants 

 You cannot use a Warm Homes loan and other government funding to pay for the same improvement.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a Warm Homes loan:

 you must own the property you’re applying for, either with or without a mortgage

 the property must be in the UK

 the property must be residential, not commercial

 Lenders may also have more eligibility rules.

 What you must use your loan for

 You must use the loan to pay for one or more of the following energy saving improvements:

 air source and ground source heat pumps

 solar photovoltaic ( PV ) panels

 battery storage systems

 heat network connections

 domestic wind turbines

 micro hydroelectric systems

 You can apply for a loan to pay for more than one improvement.

 Approved installers

 You must usually get a Microgeneration Certification Scheme ( MCS ) certified installer to do the improvement work, but you can get any installer to install heat network connections.

 You cannot do the improvement work yourself.

## How to apply

You apply to a lender that offers Warm Homes loans . You do not apply to the government.

 You can apply:

 directly to a lender

 through an installer or retailer when arranging finance for the improvement work

 through your mortgage provider

 The loan product will say if it’s funded by the government through the Warm Homes Loan Scheme.

 You will need to pass the lender’s credit and affordability assessments.

## What happens if you’re selected for an audit

After your energy saving improvements have been installed, you may be randomly selected for an audit.

 This may be a check by an auditor on behalf of the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero ( DESNZ ) or a quality check by the Microgeneration Certification Scheme ( MCS ).

 If you’re selected for an audit, it does not mean that something is wrong with your application or installation.

 Audits on behalf of DESNZ 

 You’ll be contacted by the organisation doing the audit on behalf of DESNZ . They will explain the purpose of the audit and might also arrange to visit your property.

 You might have to provide documents, for example proof of your identity or address.

 If a visit is needed, you will be given at least 48 hours’ notice. When an auditor visits, they can provide identification and details confirming that they are acting on behalf of the DESNZ Warm Homes Loan Scheme.

 Audits by the MCS 

 If you are selected for an audit by the MCS , you’ll be asked questions about the improvements you’ve had installed.

 They may also ask to arrange a visit to your property. If a visit is needed, they will give you more information about what it will involve.

## Help and support

If you have a question about loan products, application process and lending criteria, contact lenders directly.

 If you have a question about the scheme that your lender or installer cannot answer, contact the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero ( DESNZ ) Warm Homes Loan Scheme.

 DESNZ Warm Homes Loan Scheme team 
 WHLS@energysecurity.gov.uk 

 Make a complaint

 If you have a complaint about a lender or installer, speak to them directly to try to resolve the issue.

 If you cannot resolve an issue with a lender, you might be able to make a complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service .

 If you cannot resolve an issue with an installer, make a complaint to the Microgeneration Certification Scheme ( MCS ) .

 Report fraud or scams

 If you suspect fraud, scams or misuse of the Warm Homes Loan Scheme, you can report it to Warm Homes Counter Fraud.

 Warm Homes Counter Fraud 
 WHLScounterfraud@energysecurity.gov.uk
