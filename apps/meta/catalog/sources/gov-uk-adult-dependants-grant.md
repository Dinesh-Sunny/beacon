# Adult Dependants' Grant

Source: https://www.gov.uk/adult-dependants-grant
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-02-03T10:16:24+00:00

## Overview

If you’re a student and an adult depends on you financially, you may be able to apply for an Adult Dependants’ Grant of up to £3,545.

 You could be eligible if you’re either:

 in full-time higher education

 studying 120 credits or more on a year of a course funded through the Lifelong Learning Entitlement (LLE) 

 The grant:

 does not have to be paid back

 is paid on top of your other student finance

 You may have to pay back any money you are overpaid because of a change in your circumstances or course.

 You cannot get an Adult Dependants’ Grant if you’re getting a Postgraduate Loan.

## What you'll get

The maximum Adult Dependants’ Grant is £3,545.

 You do not have to pay this money back.

 Adult Dependants’ Grant will affect any income-related benefits you might get.

 What it’s based on

 The amount you get depends on:

 your income

 the adult dependant’s income

 your personal circumstances, for example if you’re married or have children

 How you’re paid

 The money is paid directly into your bank account in up to 3 instalments per year, depending on the length of your course.

## Eligibility

To get an Adult Dependants’ Grant, another adult must depend on you financially. They cannot be any of the following:

 your child

 a relative who earns more than £3,796 a year

 getting student finance

 your partner if you’re under 25 - unless you’re married or in a civil partnership

 You’re also not eligible if:

 your household income (your own taxable income plus that of anyone else living in your household) is over £15,835.98

 you’re getting a Postgraduate Loan

 If your course starts on or after 1 August 2026, the adult who depends on you must live in the UK. If they only live in the UK for part of the year, they’ll get less than if they lived there full time.

## How to apply

Fill in the Adult Dependants’ Grant section on your main student finance application - you’ll need to give estimates of your household income.

 Student Finance England will assess your application and contact you if they need more information.

 Student Finance England will contact you to tell you if you qualify and how much money you’ll get.

 Supporting information

 Student Finance England may contact you and ask for further information on your financial circumstances. This can include a copy of:

 your P60

 Child Benefit details

 financial information from your partner, for example bank statements

 Tell Student Finance England straight away if an adult who is dependant on you starts claiming student finance and you are getting an Adult Dependants’ Grant.
