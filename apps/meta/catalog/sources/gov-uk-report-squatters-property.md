# Report squatters in a council property

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-squatters-property
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:19+01:00

Report squatters in a council property to the local council

If you think someone’s squatting in a council property, contact the local council.

Squatting is a criminal offence. You may also wish to contact the police .

Available in England and Wales only
