# Report an illegal driving instructor

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-an-illegal-driving-instructor
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-03-11T13:21:05+00:00

Report someone who is not a qualified and registered driving instructor but is charging for driving lessons

It’s illegal for someone to charge for driving lessons if they are not qualified and registered - you can report them to the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ).

 The only people who can charge a fee to give driving lessons are:

 approved driving instructors - they must display a green badge in their windscreen

 trainee driving instructors with a licence - they must display a pink badge in their windscreen

 To report someone who you think is an illegal driving instructor, email or phone DVSA .
