# Check how to claim a National Insurance refund

Source: https://www.gov.uk/claim-national-insurance-refund
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-10-31T17:04:43+00:00

Find out how to claim a refund on the National Insurance (NI) you’ve paid to HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC).

Use this tool to check how to claim a refund on your National Insurance contributions from HM Revenue and Customs ( HMRC ).

 This page is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 If you’ve already claimed a refund, check when you can expect a reply .

 Find out how to claim

 There are different classes of National Insurance . You’ll be asked what class your refund is for.

 If  HMRC  has written to you about National Insurance contributions (and your letter includes a claim reference number), you can  apply for a refund of National Insurance contributions .
