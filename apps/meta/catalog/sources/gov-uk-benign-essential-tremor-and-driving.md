# Essential tremor and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/benign-essential-tremor-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-06T14:23:11+01:00

You may need to tell DVLA if you have an essential tremor - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if your essential tremor affects your ability to drive safely.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Ask your doctor if you’re not sure if your essential tremor will affect your driving.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You must tell DVLA if your condition will affect your driving.

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form B1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if your condition will affect your driving. Fill in form B1V and send to the address on the form.
