# Hodgkin's lymphoma and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hodgkins-lymphoma-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-09-16T12:40:35+01:00

You may need to tell DVLA If you have Hodgkin's lymphoma 

You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You must tell DVLA if you have Hodgkin’s lymphoma and any of the following also apply: 

 you develop problems with your brain or nervous system

 your doctor says you might not be fit to drive

 you’re restricted to certain types of vehicles or vehicles that have been adapted for you

 your medication causes side effects which could affect your driving

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form C1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA .

 Fill in form C1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
