# Apply for approval to book motorcycle, vocational and ADI qualifying tests for your pupils

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-approval-book-pupil-motorcycle-vocational-adi-tests
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-12-12T09:00:03+00:00

Apply to the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) for approval to book and manage driving and motorcycle tests for your pupils.

Driving instructors no longer book, change or cancel car driving tests. Your pupils must now do it themselves.

 You can apply for approval to book and manage motorcycle, vocational and ADI qualifying tests if you’re:

 an approved driving instructor ( ADI )

 a business that provides driving instructor training and employs ADIs

 a motorcycle approved training body ( ATB )

 a vocational driving instructor who trains lorry, bus or coach drivers

 If your application is approved, you can:

 book more than one test at the same time

 swap test appointments for your pupils

 book some types of test without giving your pupil’s details at the time

 You can only book tests for learner drivers or riders who are being taught by you or your business.

 You may lose your fee if you cancel a test at short notice or do not use it.

 How to apply for approval

 How you apply to the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ) depends on what kind of test you need to book.

 Motorcycle tests

 Apply to book motorcycle tests .

 Vocational tests

 Apply to book vocational tests .

 ADI qualifying tests

 Apply to book ADI qualifying tests .

 After you apply 

 You’ll usually get a decision by email within 10 working days.

 If your application is approved, DVSA will send you an activation code by email. To sign in to the booking service, you’ll need: 

 the activation code

 your DVSA business ID

 the postcode of your business

 Booking theory tests

 You must have been approved by DVSA to book motorcycle or vocational tests before you can book theory tests for your pupils .
