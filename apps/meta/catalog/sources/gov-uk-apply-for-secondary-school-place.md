# Apply for a secondary school place

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-for-secondary-school-place
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2018-11-26T11:01:56+00:00

Apply for a state secondary school place through your local council

You can apply for a place at a state secondary school online or by using your council’s application form.

 You apply through your local council even if you’re applying for schools in another council area.

Available in England and Wales only
