# Apply for help to improve a home with no mains gas boiler (Home Upgrade Grant)

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-home-upgrade-grant
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-11-18T13:56:47+00:00

The Home Upgrade Grant has closed.

The Home Upgrade Grant is now closed. You may be able to apply for the Warm Homes: Local Grant instead.

 The Warm Homes: Local Grant may help you make improvements to your home to make it more energy efficient.
