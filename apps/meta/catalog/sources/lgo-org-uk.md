Welcome to the Local Government & Social Care Ombudsman 

 We make decisions on complaints about councils or adult care providers in England 

 We can investigate individual complaints about councils, all adult social care providers (including care homes and home care agencies) and some other public service organisations. If we decide to investigate, we look at whether organisations have made decisions the right way.

 We are a free service

 We decide whether or not to investigate your complaint

 We are independent and do not take sides
