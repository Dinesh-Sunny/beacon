# Find your nearest number plate supplier

Source: https://www.gov.uk/number-plate-supplier
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-02-22T17:17:43+00:00

Find a business that sells number plates for your vehicle

You can only get a number plate made up from a registered number plate supplier.

 Not all suppliers sell all types of number plate.

 It can take up to 14 days for a supplier to be added or removed from this service.

The supplier will need to see original documents that:

 prove your identity (for example your passport)

 show you’re entitled to the registration number (for example the V5 vehicle registration certificate)
