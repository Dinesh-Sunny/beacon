# Report a coal mine hazard

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-coal-mine-hazard
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-11-29T18:04:52+00:00

Report a problem with a coal mine to the Mining Remediation Authority - including ground collapses, emissions and leaks from a mine, unsealed or unsafe abandoned mine entrances

Report a problem with a coal mine to the Mining Remediation Authority.

 The Mining Remediation Authority 
0800 288 4242 
Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week 
 Find out about call charges 

 Problems could include:

 a collapsed mine or mine entrance

 gas or water emissions from an abandoned mine

 an entrance to an abandoned mine that’s not sealed

 a ground collapse, for example a hole in the ground (‘sinkhole’)
