# Online Centres Network

Source: https://www.gov.uk/ukonline-centre-internet-access-computer-training
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-02-20T12:15:22+00:00

Online Centres Network offers free internet access, computer training courses for beginners and advice getting online - often free, or at low cost

Online Centres Network provides access to the internet and help using it. Some centres also offer training courses. These services are usually free. Enter your postcode to find centres near you.
