# Apply to become a driving instructor

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-to-become-a-driving-instructor
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-09-06T15:06:09+01:00

Apply to start qualifying as an approved driving instructor (ADI) with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA)

Start the process to become an approved driving instructor ( ADI ) if you’re:

 applying for the first time

 reapplying after failing the qualifying tests

 reapplying after being an ADI before

 Check you can become a driving instructor before you apply.

 There’s a different process to apply to become an ADI in Northern Ireland .
