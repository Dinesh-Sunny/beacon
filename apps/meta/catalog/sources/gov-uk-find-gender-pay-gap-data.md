# Find and compare gender pay gap data

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-gender-pay-gap-data
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2018-04-03T13:59:04+01:00

Search gender pay gap data for organisations in the UK, and download data by organisation size or business sector  

Use this service to find gender pay gap data for organisations in the UK.

 The gender pay gap is the average difference between how much men and women are paid in an organisation.

 You can find and compare:

 the average difference between men and women’s hourly and bonus pay

 the percentage of men and women in the highest, middle and lowest pay groups in a company

 You can also download data by organisation size or business sector.

 Only companies with more than 250 employees have to declare their gender pay gap data.

 Find out if you need to report your company’s data .
