# Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hypertrophic-cardiomyopathy-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T13:41:22+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and drive a bus, coach or lorry - download the correct form to let them know


You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not need to tell DVLA if you have hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if you have hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

 Fill in form VOCH1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
