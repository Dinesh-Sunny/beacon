# Cognitive problems and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/cognitive-problems-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-05T14:42:28+01:00

Find out if you need to tell DVLA about cognitive problems and how to report your condition

You must tell DVLA if you’re diagnosed with cognitive problems and it affects your ability to drive safely.

 If your driving is not affected by cognitive problems, you do not need to tell DVLA . Ask your doctor if you’re unsure.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 There’s a different process for driving with dementia .

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form CG1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form CG1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
