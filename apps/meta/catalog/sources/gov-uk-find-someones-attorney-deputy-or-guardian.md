# Find out if someone has an attorney, deputy or guardian acting for them

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-someones-attorney-deputy-or-guardian
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-12-11T17:47:20+00:00

Apply to search the Office of the Public Guardian registers to see if they have a Lasting Power of Attorney, deputy or guardian acting on their behalf

Apply to search the Office of the Public Guardian ( OPG ) registers to see if someone has another person acting on their behalf.

 This can be:

 an attorney under a lasting power of attorney 

 an attorney under an enduring power of attorney 

 a court appointed deputy 

 a guardian for someone who is missing appointed by the High Court

 This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 You need to fill in a form to search the register . This is a free service.

 Send your completed form to:

 opgregistersearches@publicguardian.gov.uk 

 You can contact OPG by post:

Office of the Public Guardian PO Box 16185 Birmingham B2 2WH

 Document Exchange (DX) address:

Office of the Public Guardian DX 744240 Birmingham 79

 OPG will respond within 5 working days. It may take longer if they contact you by post.
