# Change the details on your Blue Badge

Source: https://www.gov.uk/change-blue-badge-details
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-01-24T14:05:48+00:00

Change the details on your Blue Badge and get a replacement if your name has changed 

Contact your local council to change the details on your Blue Badge.

 You can ask for a replacement badge if your name has changed.

 You can carry on using your old badge until the new one arrives.

 There’s a different way to change your details if you’re in Northern Ireland .
