# Hypoglycaemia and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hypoglycaemia-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T13:40:25+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have hypoglycaemia - download the correct form to let them know


You must tell DVLA if you have hypoglycaemia.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form DIAB1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Read the guide to filling in form DIAB1 .

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in the form that’s appropriate for how your diabetes is controlled:

 insulin 

 sulphonylurea or glinide tablets (or both) 

 other tablets or non-insulin injections 

 Ask your nurse or doctor if you do not know what type of medication you’re on.

 Send your completed form to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Read leaflet INS186 to apply for vocational entitlement to drive larger vehicles (C1, C1E, D1, DIE, C, CE, D or DE).
