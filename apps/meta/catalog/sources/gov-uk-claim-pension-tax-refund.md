# Check how to claim a tax refund on your pension

Source: https://www.gov.uk/claim-pension-tax-refund
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2026-09-25T15:50:36+01:00

Find out how to claim your tax refund including which form to use for example a P50Z, P53, P53Z or P55 form.

You may be able to claim a tax refund on a pension if you’ve:

 stopped working and taken all your pension

 taken a small pension lump sum

 flexibly accessed all, or some, of your pension

 had income from a pension annuity

 If you’ve already claimed a tax refund, check when you can expect a reply .

 Before you start

 To check how to claim, you’ll need:

 your National Insurance number

 the amount of pension payment and tax taken from it

 details of any other taxable income you expect to receive

 payment details for you or your nominee — these will be used if you’re due a refund

 To claim online, you’ll need to sign in to use the service. If you do not already have sign in details, you’ll be able to create them.
