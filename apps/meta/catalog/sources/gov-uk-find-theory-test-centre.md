Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-04-25T13:30:06+01:00

Find the details of your nearest driving theory test centres

Use your postcode to find your nearest driving theory test centres. You can:

 get a map and directions to the centre

 check any special instructions for using the centre

You take your practical driving test at a different centre. Find a driving test centre where you can take a test.
