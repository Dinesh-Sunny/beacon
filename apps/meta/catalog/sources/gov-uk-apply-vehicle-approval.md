# Apply for Individual Vehicle Approval (IVA)

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-vehicle-approval
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-01-27T12:00:06+00:00

Apply for an Individual Vehicle Approval (IVA) test for cars, goods vehicles, buses and coaches, and trailers on the manage vehicle testing service

Apply for an Individual Vehicle Approval test for:

 passenger cars

 goods vehicles

 buses and coaches

 trailers

 special purpose vehicles, for example, vehicles specially designed to hold a wheelchair

 You can use this service for single and multiple vehicles.

 Sign in or create an account

 You need to sign in or create an account to use this service.

To use the service you’ll need:

 Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) or chassis number

 vehicle registration number (if registered)
