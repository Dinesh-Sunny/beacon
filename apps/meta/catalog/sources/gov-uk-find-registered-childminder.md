# Find a registered childminder

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-registered-childminder
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-11-05T09:27:23+00:00

Find a registered childminder through your local council or a registered childminding agency. 

Childminding agencies

 You can also look for a childminder through the following registered childminding agencies: 

 Suffolk Childcare Agency - for childminders across England

 Tiney - for childminders across England 

 @Home Childcare - for childminders in East and West Midlands, Greater Manchester, South Yorkshire, as well as Bedfordshire, Gloucestershire and London Borough of Havering

 Unique Support Early Years Agency - for childminders in Essex, Gloucestershire, Hampshire and Kent

You can search for a registered childminder through your local council.

Go to your local council’s website to find out about:

 registered childminders in your area

 early years education and family support

 There’s a different way to find registered childminders, early years education or family support in Northern Ireland .

 You can also contact a registered childminding agency to find a local childminder. These agencies cover England only.
