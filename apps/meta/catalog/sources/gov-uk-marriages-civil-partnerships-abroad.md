# Getting married or registering a civil partnership abroad

Source: https://www.gov.uk/marriages-civil-partnerships-abroad
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-06-14T13:59:21+01:00

## Check if you can get married or register a civil partnership abroad

Whether you can get married or register a civil partnership in your chosen country depends on local laws.

 You might not be able to do this if you or your partner:

 are not nationals of the country or do not meet residency requirements

 want a same-sex marriage or civil partnership and these are not recognised there

 do not meet legal, cultural or religious requirements

 Check the laws of your chosen country

 You need to check the laws in the country where you want to marry or register a civil partnership.

 You’ll need to:

 check if you’re allowed to get married or register a civil partnership there

 find out the process you need to follow

 check which documents you’ll need to prove you’re free to marry or register a civil partnership 

 You can get advice from:

 the person conducting the ceremony

 the authority that registers marriages or civil partnerships in that country

 a wedding planner

 If you cannot get married or register a civil partnership in your chosen country

 In some countries, you may be able to marry or register a civil partnership at a British embassy, consulate or high commission. This is called a ‘consular marriage’ or ‘consular civil partnership’.

 Check which countries offer consular marriage and consular civil partnership and the processes you’ll need to follow.

 Convert a same-sex civil partnership to a marriage

 You may be able to convert a UK same-sex civil partnership into a marriage in some countries abroad.

 You cannot convert an opposite-sex civil partnership into a marriage abroad.

## Get proof you're free to marry or register a civil partnership abroad

Some countries may ask for documents to prove you’re free to get married or register a civil partnership abroad.

 You can use this service to:

 check what documents you might need - for example a certificate of no impediment or a marital status affirmation

 apply and pay for the documents

 Who can use this service

 You must have a valid British passport to use this service.

 Before you start

 Make sure you’ve checked that you can get married or register a civil partnership in your chosen country and you understand the processes you’ll need to follow.

 You’ll be asked to confirm this when you apply.

## Getting your marriage or civil partnership certificate

When you get married or register a civil partnership abroad, the authorities in your chosen country will register it in their national civil records.

 Speak to the person conducting your ceremony to find out how to get your marriage or civil partnership certificate.

 You may want to get your foreign certificate translated. This can be helpful if you plan to use it outside of the country where it was issued.

 Find a translator abroad .

 You cannot get a UK marriage or civil partnership certificate if your marriage or civil partnership happened abroad. Only UK marriages and civil partnerships are registered in the UK.

 If you want a UK marriage or civil partnership certificate, you can choose to have a legal ceremony in the UK and a commitment ceremony abroad.

 A foreign marriage or civil partnership is recognised in the UK if:

 it was lawful in the country where it took place

 it would be allowed under UK law

 Consular marriage and civil partnership certificates

 If you get a consular marriage or consular civil partnership at a British embassy, high commission or consulate abroad, it will be registered in the UK civil records.

 You’ll also get a UK marriage or civil partnership certificate.

## Get help abroad

If you need help getting married or registering a civil partnership abroad, you can:

 find a lawyer 

 find a translator or interpreter 

 find a notary (a notary is a lawyer who can confirm if legal documents are authentic)
