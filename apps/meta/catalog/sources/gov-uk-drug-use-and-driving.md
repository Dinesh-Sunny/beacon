# Drug use and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/drug-use-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-06T14:24:22+01:00

If you've used illegal drugs or misused drugs you must tell DVLA - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if you’ve used illegal drugs or misused prescription drugs.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form DG1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 In Northern Ireland you must tell the DVA . Fill in form DL1. 

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form DG1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 In Northern Ireland you must tell the DVA . Fill in form DLM1.
