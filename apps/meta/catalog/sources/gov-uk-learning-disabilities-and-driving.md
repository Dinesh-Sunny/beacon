# Learning disabilities and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/learning-disabilities-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T13:43:36+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have a learning disability - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if you have a learning disability that affects your ability to drive safely.

 You do not need to tell DVLA if you have learning difficulties, for example dyslexia.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form A1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form A1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
