Scottish Welfare Fund - help with living costs 
 What the Scottish Welfare Fund is

 Last updated 
 4 Jun 2025 

 The Scottish Welfare Fund is made up of 2 different grants.
 To apply for a grant from the Scottish Welfare Fund you must be:

 16 or older

 on a low income or getting certain benefits

 You can only apply through your local council. You cannot apply through the Scottish Government.
 There are different grants you can apply for depending on your circumstances. You do not need to pay these grants back.
 You can apply for a:

 Crisis Grant – to help with an unexpected emergency like a fire, flood or losing your job

 Community Care Grant – to help you or someone you care for live independently
