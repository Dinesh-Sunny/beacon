# Record Driver CPC training you provide

Publisher updated: 2015-09-25T12:14:05+01:00

Record the details of candidates who have completed a Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (CPC) periodic training course

Record the details of candidates who have completed a Driver Certificate of Professional Competence ( CPC ) periodic training course. You must be an approved training centre.

You’ll need your:

 user ID

 password

 Read the guide to recording Driver CPC periodic training .

 Giving another person access

 You can register another person to use the Driver CPC training database if you need them to enter training records. They will not need to register with the Government Gateway.

 Paying to record training

 You have to pay to record training details. The fee is £1.25 per driver, per hour of training.

 You can pay in 2 ways:

 by debit or credit card

 by debit from a pre-funded account

 Setting up a pre-funded account lets you deposit funds with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ) in advance and pay the fee by debiting the amount directly from your account.
