# Estimate your Income Tax for the current year

Source: https://www.gov.uk/estimate-income-tax
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-04-06T15:00:44+01:00

Estimate how much Income Tax and National Insurance you can expect to pay for the current tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027).

Use this service to estimate how much Income Tax and National Insurance you should pay for the current tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027).

 This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 This service is for employees who pay tax and National Insurance each month through their employer.

 It tells you what your take-home pay will be after: 

 paying tax 

 making any pension contributions 

 making any student loan repayments

 If you’re registered for Self Assessment, the Self Assessment tax calculator can help you estimate your tax bill.

 You may be able to claim a refund if you’ve paid too much tax.

If you have more than one job, use the calculator once for each job.

 You can also use the HMRC app to estimate how much Income Tax you should pay for the current year.

 You cannot get an estimate from this service if your only income is from state benefits , such as the State Pension.

 If your income is above £35,000 and you get the Winter Fuel Payment (or Pension Age Winter Heating Payment in Scotland), you’ll need to pay it back. You do this by paying more tax the following year. This service will not show this extra amount of Income Tax. You can read more about paying back the Winter Fuel Payment .

 There are different ways to:

 check your Income Tax payments for the current year 

 check how much you paid last year (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026) 

 estimate how much you should have paid in a previous year 

 You can check you’ve paid the right tax by contacting HMRC or by getting help from an accountant .
