# Challenge a parking fine

Source: https://www.gov.uk/appeal-parking-fine
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-03-24T13:22:23+00:00

Contact a local council to challenge a parking fine or parking ticket - if it’s a penalty charge notice (PCN)

Challenge a penalty charge notice ( PCN ) for parking with the local council that issued it.

 Use the council’s postcode.

 If you get a text message from Department for Transport (DfT) telling you to pay an unpaid parking fine, this is a scam. DfT does not issue parking fines. Read advice on what to do if you receive a scam message .

Available in England and Wales only.

 You have 28 days to challenge a PCN . If you do it within 14 days and your challenge is rejected, you may only have to pay 50% of the fine.

 If your parking fine is not from a council

 Check the parking ticket to find out who issued it and how to challenge it .
