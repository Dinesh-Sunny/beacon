# Apply for a company digital tachograph card

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-company-tachograph-card
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-03-12T14:22:00+00:00

Contact DVLA to get a digital tachograph company smart card - change, replace, renew a card

As an operator, you need a tachograph company card to download data from the digital or smart tachographs in your vehicles.

 There’s a different way to apply for a card if you work as a driver .

 Use this service to:

 apply for a first digital tachograph company card

 apply for additional company cards

 renew an existing card

 replace lost, stolen, damaged or faulty cards

 change the name or address on your company cards

Contact the Digital Tachograph Team for help with the online service.
