# Get help with the cost of living from your local council

Source: https://www.gov.uk/cost-living-help-local-council
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2022-11-29T16:22:13+00:00

Help you can get from your council’s Crisis and Resilience Fund if you cannot afford to pay for essentials like energy bills and food.

You may be able to get help with essential costs from your local council. This could help if you’re struggling to afford things like:

 energy and water bills

 food

 essential items

 housing costs

 In England, this support is known as the ‘Crisis and Resilience Fund’. If you live in Scotland or Wales, you may be able to get help from:

 the Scottish Welfare Fund (in Scotland)

 the Discretionary Assistance Fund (in Wales)

 Your council may also offer food vouchers to families during the school holidays.

 Who the Crisis and Resilience Fund is for

 Funding is aimed at anyone who’s vulnerable or cannot pay for essentials. You do not have to be getting benefits to get help from your local council.

 If you get benefits, they will not be affected if you get a payment from the Crisis and Resilience Fund.

 Find out about schemes in your area

 Councils decide how to run their schemes. There may be differences in:

 eligibility criteria

 if or how you need to apply

 who money is given to

 For example, some councils share out money through local charities and community groups and some limit household applications to one per year.

 Check with your local council to find out what support is available.

 Other help you can get

 You can also:

 check what benefits and financial support you can get 

 get council tax support if you’re on a low income or get certain benefits

 apply for a discretionary housing payment if you live in Scotland or in Wales
