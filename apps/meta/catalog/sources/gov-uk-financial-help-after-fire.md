# House fires: financial help and fire safety

Source: https://www.gov.uk/financial-help-after-fire
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-01-10T13:52:07+00:00

Your council can help you after a fire in your home - Council, emergency housing, Red Cross, fire safety

The Red Cross and your local council can help with accommodation and other needs in an emergency.

 Emergency help

 Contact the Red Cross for emergency help with things like accommodation, clothing and food, as well as help dealing with insurance companies.

 If you have nowhere to stay

 Contact your local council . They may be able to help you with emergency housing .

 Fire safety

 Get fire safety advice on the Fire Kills page, including information on smoke alarms, making your home safe and help for people with disabilities.
