# Apply for an allotment

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-allotment
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:17+01:00

Rent an allotment through your council to grow your own fruit and vegetables - you can be allocated a plot or added to a waiting list

Contact your local council to apply for an allotment near you. They will either allocate you a plot or, in many cases, add your name to a waiting list.

Available in England and Wales only
