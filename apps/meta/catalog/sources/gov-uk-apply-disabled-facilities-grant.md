# Apply for a Disabled Facilities Grant

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-disabled-facilities-grant
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2022-02-16T14:10:12+00:00

Apply for financial help to make your home suitable for a disabled person

You can apply to your local council for a disabled facilities grant. The grant helps you adapt your home to make it suitable for a disabled person.

Find out how to apply if you’re in Northern Ireland

Available in England and Wales only
