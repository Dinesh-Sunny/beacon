# Find a soldier's will

Source: https://www.gov.uk/probate-search
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-01-06T13:39:06+00:00

Search for the will of a soldier who died while serving in the British armed forces between 1850 and 1986

Search for the will of a soldier who died while serving in the British armed forces between 1850 and 1986.

You’ll need:

 the soldier’s last name and year of death to search for a will

 to register for the service with an email address

 to pay £16 to access a will

 You will not always get a complete will - sometimes soldiers only leave letters or brief instructions.
