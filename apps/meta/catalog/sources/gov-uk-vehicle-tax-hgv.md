# Apply to tax a Heavy Goods Vehicle (HGV)

Source: https://www.gov.uk/vehicle-tax-hgv
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-10-31T00:01:07+00:00

To apply for an HGV vehicle licence download and fill in form V85 and apply in person at a Post Office that deals with vehicle tax.

Fill in form V85 and apply in person at a Post Office that deals with vehicle tax.

 You need HGV tax for lorries that weigh more than 3.5 tonnes when loaded.

 You need to take the vehicle log book (V5C). You can apply for a replacement if you do not have one.

 This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 Tax rates for HGVs 

 The amount of tax you need to pay depends on your vehicle’s tax band and whether it has road-friendly suspension.
