Fetched: 2026-11-04

Find a compulsory basic training (CBT) course to learn to ride a motorcycle or moped

Find your nearest motorcycle training schools running the compulsory basic training ( CBT ) course for motorcycles and mopeds.

The training school sets the course price. It depends on where you do the training and if you bring your own moped or motorcycle.

 Northern Ireland

 Contact the Driver and Vehicle Agency ( DVA ) to check if a motorcycle instructor or CBT course is approved in Northern Ireland.
