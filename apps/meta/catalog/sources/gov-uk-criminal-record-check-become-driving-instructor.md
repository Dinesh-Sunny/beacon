# Get a criminal record check to become a driving instructor

Source: https://www.gov.uk/criminal-record-check-become-driving-instructor
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-10-19T13:10:07+01:00

Get a criminal record check if you're applying to become an approved driving instructor (ADI)

Get a criminal record check if you’re applying to become an approved driving instructor ( ADI ) for the first time.

 You cannot use one you have already - you must get a new one.

 Use a different service to get a criminal record check if you’re applying to:

 renew your ADI registration 

 become an ADI in Northern Ireland 

 Before you start

 Check if you can become a driving instructor before you apply.

 It costs £5.40 to apply. You can pay with a debit or credit card.

 You’ll need to provide documents to prove your identity, for example:

 your passport

 your driving licence

 a utility bill that’s less than 3 months old

 a bank statement that’s less than 3 months old

 Apply online

If you cannot verify your identity online, the service will tell you how to do this at a Post Office. It costs £12 to verify your identity at a Post Office.

 If you need help with your application, contact Complete Background Screening (CBS) .

 What happens next

 Your criminal record check will be sent to you by post.

 If your record contains criminal information, DVSA will also contact you.
