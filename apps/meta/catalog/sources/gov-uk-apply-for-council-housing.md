# Apply for council housing

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-for-council-housing
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-10-02T13:53:39+01:00

Apply for council housing through your local council - you'll usually have to join a waiting list

You can apply for council housing through your local council. You’ll usually have to join a waiting list and you’re not guaranteed to get a property.
