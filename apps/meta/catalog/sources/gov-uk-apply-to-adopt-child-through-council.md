# Apply to adopt a child through your council

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-to-adopt-child-through-council
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-05-12T19:03:14+01:00

Apply to adopt a child through your local council if you are 21 or over and can offer a stable home to a child

If you’re at least 21 years old and can provide a permanent, stable and caring home, you can apply to adopt a child.

You can also apply through a voluntary adoption agency .

Available in England and Wales only.
