# Apply to do work on your home if you're in a conservation area

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-work-home-conservation-area
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-12-11T16:14:37+00:00

Check with your local council before doing building work on your home if you live in a conservation area

If you live in a conservation area, you’ll need to check with your local council before doing building work on your home.

 There’s a different way to check if you live in a National Park (for example the Lake District).

available in England and Wales only
