# Aortic stenosis and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/aortic-stenosis-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-10-14T17:00:19+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have aortic stenosis - download the form to let them know


You must tell DVLA about your aortic stenosis if one of the following applies:

 it can make you feel dizzy

 you sometimes lose consciousness

 your doctor has told you it may affect your ability to drive

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form H1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form VOCH1 and send it to the address on the form.
