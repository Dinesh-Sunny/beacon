# Sign in to your Universal Credit account

Source: https://www.gov.uk/sign-in-universal-credit
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-08-23T16:17:23+01:00

Sign in to your Universal Credit account - report a change, add a note to your journal, see your payments and do other tasks 

Sign in to your Universal Credit account to:

 apply for an advance on your first payment

 see your statement

 report a change in circumstances

 see when your next payment will be

 see your claimant commitment

 check your to-do list (for example, if your work coach has booked an appointment with you)

 send messages to your case manager or work coach using your journal

 It might take a few days for your case manager or work coach to reply. You’ll usually get a text or email if your journal or to-do list is updated.

 Use the username and password you set up when you applied for Universal Credit. You can ask for a reminder if you’re not sure.

 This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .
