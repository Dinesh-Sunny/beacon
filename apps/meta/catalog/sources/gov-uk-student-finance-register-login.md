# Student finance login

Source: https://www.gov.uk/student-finance-register-login
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-11-13T15:22:22+00:00

Your student finance online account - check payment dates, track an application, change details, reset password, find customer reference numbers. 

Use your online account to:

 view statements and letters from Student Finance England ( SFE )

 track an application

 check when payments are due

 update personal or application details

 find your customer reference number ( CRN )

 apply for student finance as a new or continuing student

 You can also use this service if you’re supporting a student finance application and SFE has asked for details of your household income.

 Continue to your account 

 If you cannot apply for student finance online

 You can use a form to apply for student finance. If your course starts:

 before 1 January 2027, use the student finance form finder 

 on or after 1 January 2027, use your online account to download forms or contact SFE 

 If you want to apply online but cannot use a computer without help, contact SFE .
