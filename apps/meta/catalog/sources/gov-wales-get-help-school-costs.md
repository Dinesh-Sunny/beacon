 The Welsh Government is offering support with Free School Meals and the School Essentials Grant, allowing children to focus on learning and fun, not finance.

 Universal Primary Free School Meals

 All children in primary school are now entitled to a free school meal.

 School Essentials Grant

 You could get up to £200 for School Essentials for your child and extra funding for their school.

 If you receive certain benefits your child may be eligible for Free School Meals and other support.

 School Essentials Grant
 Your child could be eligible for financial help of up to £200 for:
 school uniform, including coats and footwear
 school activities: this could include learning a musical instrument, sports kit and other equipment for after school activities
 classroom essentials: this could include pens, pencils and school bags

 Free School Meals
 Secondary school
 Welsh Government is expanding free school meals to all secondary school learners in households claiming Universal Credit, with no income limit, starting with children in years 7 and 8. 
 If you receive certain benefits or if your financial circumstances have changed recently, check your eligibility for Free School Meals , the School Essentials Grant and extra funding for your school .

 Pupil Development Grant
 When you register for Free School Meals, your school could get additional funding.
 Find out more about the extra funding available for your school through the Pupil Development Grant .
 Education Maintenance Allowance (EMA)
 Is your child 16-18 years old and continuing their education in school or college?
 EMA offers weekly £40 payments (paid every two weeks) directly to eligible students to help with costs such as travel, books, and equipment.
 Check eligibility and apply online in minutes .
