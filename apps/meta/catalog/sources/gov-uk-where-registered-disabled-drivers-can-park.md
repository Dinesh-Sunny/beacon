 Using your Blue Badge abroad

 The Blue Badge is recognised in some European countries and allows you to make use of the same parking concessions as the country’s own citizens with disability.

 Parking concessions for holders of a disabled parking card differ from country to country - check the rules in the country you’re visiting .
