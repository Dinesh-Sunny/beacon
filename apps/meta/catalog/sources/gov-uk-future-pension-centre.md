Future Pension Centre contact details - for pension forecasts, statements, advice and enquiries.

Contact the Future Pension Centre for questions about the State Pension or to ask for a forecast.

 This page is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 You can also check your State Pension forecast online to find out how much you could get and how you can increase it.

 Future Pension Centre helpline

 If you used the call back request form to pay voluntary National Insurance contributions, you do not need to contact the helpline. DWP Retirement Services will contact you.

Telephone from outside the UK: +44 (0)191 218 3600
