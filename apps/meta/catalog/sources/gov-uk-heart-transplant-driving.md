# Heart transplants and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/heart-transplant-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-10-22T12:44:24+01:00

Find out when you need to tell DVLA if you have had a heart transplant - download the correct forms to tell DVLA

You may need to tell DVLA if you have had a heart transplant.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. If you’re involved in an accident, you may be prosecuted.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not need to tell DVLA , but you should stop driving for at least 1 month and only start driving again when your doctor tells you that you are safe to do so.

 Read more about car or motorcycle drivers with heart conditions .

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA . Fill in form VOCH1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
