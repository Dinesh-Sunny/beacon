# Apply to foster a child through your council

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-foster-child-council
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-02-15T15:46:57+00:00

Apply to foster a child through the social services department of your local council.

Some local councils recruit foster carers jointly through a regional recruitment hub. If your council is part of a recruitment hub, you’ll be directed to the appropriate website.

Contact your local council if you’re interested in becoming a foster carer.

 Some local councils recruit foster carers jointly through a regional recruitment hub. If your council is part of a recruitment hub, you’ll be directed to the appropriate website.

 There’s a different way to apply: 

 if you live in Scotland  

 if you live in Wales  

 if you live in Northern Ireland
