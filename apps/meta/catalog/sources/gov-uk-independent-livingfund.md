# Independent Living Fund

Source: https://www.gov.uk/independent-livingfund
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-07-01T00:01:01+01:00

The Independent Living Fund (ILF) provided funds to help disabled people live an independent life - closed to new applications, reviews of existing payments

The Independent Living Fund ( ILF ) has closed - you cannot make a new claim.

 England and Wales

 Your local council is responsible for ILF payments.

 Scotland and Northern Ireland

 ILF Scotland is responsible for ILF payments in Northern Ireland and Scotland.

 Repayments

 If you have any questions about ILF repayments, email the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) at cru.customerfeedback@dwp.gov.uk .
