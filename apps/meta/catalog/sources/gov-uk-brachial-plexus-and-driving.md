# Brachial plexus injury and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/brachial-plexus-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-05T10:55:49+01:00

Tell DVLA if you have a brachial plexus injury - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if you have a brachial plexus injury.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form G1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form G1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
