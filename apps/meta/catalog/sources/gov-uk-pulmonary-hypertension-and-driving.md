# Pulmonary arterial hypertension and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/pulmonary-hypertension-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2020-03-04T14:32:04+00:00

You must tell DVLA if you have pulmonary arterial hypertension and you're receiving treatment from a specialist centre - download the correct form to let them know.

You must tell DVLA if you have pulmonary arterial hypertension and you’re receiving treatment from an NHS specialist centre .

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also Fill in form PAH1 and send it to DVLA. The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form PAH1V and send it to DVLA. The address is on the form.
