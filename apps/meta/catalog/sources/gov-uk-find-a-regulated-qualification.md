# Find a regulated qualification

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-a-regulated-qualification
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2014-11-27T11:38:28+00:00

Find out about a qualification, including if it’s regulated and how it compares to other qualifications.

Search the Register of Regulated Qualifications to:

 find out if a qualification is regulated (officially recognised)

 see detailed information about a qualification

 compare similar qualifications

 The register includes GCSE, A level, AS level and vocational (work related) qualifications in England and Northern Ireland.

You can also search for regulated awarding organisations .

 Visit the National Careers Service to find out which qualifications you need to get into a specific career.

 Wales and Scotland

 You can also find qualifications regulated in:

 Wales - at Qualifications in Wales 

 Scotland - at SQA Accreditation
