# Motorcycle trainer fees

Source: https://www.gov.uk/motorcycle-trainer-fees
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2014-11-26T12:33:06+00:00

Fees for becoming one of the different types a motorcycle trainer and registering with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA)

There’s no charge to take the compulsory basic training (CBT) and direct access scheme (DAS) instructor assessments, but there are fees for other types of motorcycle trainer tests and registration.

 Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ) special test fee

 As a fully qualified motorcycle trainer you can take the DVSA special test if you want your skills tested to the highest standard.

 It costs £163 to take the DVSA special test.

 DVSA enhanced rider scheme trainer fees

 You have to pay a fee to take the qualifying tests and then register to become a DVSA enhanced rider scheme trainer so you can give enhanced rider scheme training.

 There are different fees for motorcycle trainers in Northern Ireland.
