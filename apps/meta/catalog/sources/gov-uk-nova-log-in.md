Sign in to use the Notification of Vehicle Arrivals (NOVA) service 

Use the NOVA service to tell HM Revenue and Customs ( HMRC ) you’ve brought a vehicle into the UK.

 You can use the service if you’re:

 a VAT-registered business that does not use the secure registration scheme 

 not VAT-registered and you’ve imported a vehicle from the EU into Northern Ireland

 Online services may be slow during busy times. Check if there are any problems with this service .

You’ll need to sign in to use this service. If you do not already have sign in details, you’ll be able to create them.

 After you’ve made a NOVA vehicle arrival notification, you can see updates from HMRC in your secure communications area .
