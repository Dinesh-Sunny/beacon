# Find a Driver CPC part 3a (off-road exercises) test provider

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-driver-cpc-part-3a-test-provider
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2021-11-25T14:38:26+00:00

Find an approved test provider for Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (CPC) part 3a tests: heavy goods vehicle (HGV) and bus driving qualifications.

The Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (CPC) part 3a test is one of the tests you need to pass to become a heavy goods vehicle (HGV) or bus driver.

 Enter your postcode to find an approved test provider to take your Driver CPC part 3a test with.

 An approved test provider can charge up to £40 for the test. This does not include the cost of training or hiring a vehicle.

 If there are no test providers near you, you can book a test with the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ). A test with DVSA costs £40.
