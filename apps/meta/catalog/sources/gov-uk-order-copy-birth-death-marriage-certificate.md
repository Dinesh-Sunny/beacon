# Order a birth, death, marriage or civil partnership certificate

Source: https://www.gov.uk/order-copy-birth-death-marriage-certificate
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-06-09T16:45:02+01:00

Order an official birth, adoption, death, marriage or civil partnership certificate from the General Register Office (GRO) if you need a copy or want to research your family tree.

You need to register on the General Register Office ( GRO ) website to get a copy of a birth, adoption, death, marriage or civil partnership certificate in England and Wales.

 It takes around 10 minutes and you’ll need a debit or credit card.

 You can also use this service to:

 research your family tree (order with the GRO index reference number )

 support a passport application

 If you’re ordering a certificate for a passport application, you can get it sent to HM Passport Office ( HMPO ). You’ll need your application reference number (usually starting ‘PEX’) and the address HMPO provided.

 There’s a different process for getting certificates in Scotland and getting certificates in Northern Ireland .

 Start now 

 Certificate fees

 Certificates cost £12.50 and are sent 4 days after you apply.

 If you do not have a GRO index reference number , you’ll have to pay £3.50 extra for each search. Certificates are sent 15 working days after you apply.

 If you need the certificate sooner, you can use the priority service for £38.50. It’ll be sent the next working day if you order by 4pm.

 Extra copies are the same price.

 Get help ordering a certificate

 Contact the General Register Office for help ordering a certificate or questions about an order.

 You can use the online form to contact the GRO or contact them by telephone.

 Other ways to apply

 Ordering by post

 To order certificates by post, fill in the relevant GRO certificate application and send it to the address on the form.

 Ordering from a local register office

 You can also order certificates from the local register office where the birth, death or marriage was registered.

 Registration of births or deaths abroad

 If you’ve registered a birth or death with the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office ( FCDO ) Overseas Registration Unit, you cannot get a copy from the GRO until the November of the following year.

 If you need a copy sooner, email the name of the registered person, the registration date and reference number to the Overseas Registration Unit.

 Overseas Registration Unit 
 Overseas.RegistrationUnit@fcdo.gov.uk 

 You’ll get a reply telling you how to get your copy and the cost - usually £50 plus courier fees.
