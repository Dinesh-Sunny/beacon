# University Clearing through UCAS

Source: https://www.gov.uk/university-clearing-through-ucas
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-06-05T15:45:28+01:00

If you do not get a place on your chosen university course, you can apply for other courses through the UCAS system of Clearing.

You can apply for other courses through Clearing if you do not get a place on your chosen university course.

 You may find another course through Clearing if:

 you’re flexible about the course you study

 you have reasonable exam results

 You may still get a place on one of your chosen courses if you miss your grades by a narrow margin.

 Who can use Clearing

 You can use Clearing if:

 you’ve completed a UCAS application 

 you do not get any offers

 your offers are not confirmed

 You can also use Clearing if you do not want any of the offers you’ve received. Find UCAS guidance on how to turn down a place to enter Clearing .

 If you apply for your course after 30 June, you must apply through Clearing.

 Find a course through Clearing

 Clearing runs from early July to October each year. You can find out which courses have vacancies through UCAS from early July. Not all universities or courses have vacancies, and some may be filled quickly.

 You can contact universities and colleges about getting a place once you’ve got your exam results.

 Accepting an offer through Clearing

 You can only apply for one place at a time through Clearing.

 If you accept a place, you’ll be sent confirmation by UCAS and the university or college.

 If you change your mind about an offer you’ve accepted, you can apply for another place elsewhere. Before you reapply through Clearing to a new university or college, get confirmation they will offer you a place.

 Help and advice

 Contact UCAS for advice if your exam results were higher or lower than you expected.
