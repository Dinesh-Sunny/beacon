# Apply for a vehicle test or certificate for a coach or bus

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-vehicle-test-certificate-coach-bus
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-01-27T12:00:06+00:00

Apply for tests or certificates for your coach or bus including a Certificate of Initial Fitness (COIF), accessibility certificate and alteration forms 

Use this service if you:

 need a Certificate of Initial Fitness ( COIF ) to register your coach or bus as a Public Service Vehicle ( PSV )

 need an Accessibility Certificate to carry disabled passengers

 need to apply for a replacement certificate

 have made a specification change to your coach or bus

 want permission to drive up to 100 km/h in France or Germany

 Sign in or create an account

 You need to sign in or create an account to use this service.

Before you start, you need to download and fill in a test application form .

 Use the service to upload your completed form.
