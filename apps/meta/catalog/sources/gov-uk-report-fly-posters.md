# Report fly-posting

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-fly-posters
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:22+01:00

Report fly-posting to your local council - posters, stickers or bills put up without permission of the property owner

Fly-posters usually advertise events with posters, stickers or bills that are put up without the permission of the property owner. Fly-posting is a criminal offence. Report fly-posters to your local council or the police.

Available in England and Wales only
