How to become a large goods vehicle (LGV) instructor to provide training to people learning to drive lorries

You must have had a large goods vehicle ( LGV ) driving licence for at least 3 years to supervise a learner driver.

 You do not need to take formal training or to be registered to teach someone to drive an LGV .

 Register as an instructor voluntarily

 You can apply to join the:

 national register of LGV instructors 

 national vocational driving instructors register 

 register of logistics instructors 

 These registers have replaced the DVSA voluntary register of LGV instructors.
