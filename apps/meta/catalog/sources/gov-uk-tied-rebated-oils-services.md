# Sign in to tied and rebated oils excise services

Source: https://www.gov.uk/tied-rebated-oils-services
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2014-12-10T18:23:51+00:00

Access the HMRC's Rebated and Tied Oils Enquiry Services, Rebated Oils and Returns Internet Upload Service

Sign in to the following HM Revenue and Customs ( HMRC ) excise services for dealers in tied and rebated (controlled) oils:

 Rebated Oils Enquiry Service

 Rebated Oils Returns Internet Upload Service

 Tied Oil Enquiry Service

Online services may be slow during busy times. Check if there are any problems with the rebated oils or tied oils services.

 These services are for registered dealers in rebated (controlled) oils and tied oils to submit returns and check customers’ approval numbers.

 You’ll need to sign in to use this service. If you do not already have sign in details, you’ll be able to create them. Your organisation must be approved under the relevant scheme when you create sign in details.
