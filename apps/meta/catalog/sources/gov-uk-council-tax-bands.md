# Check your Council Tax band

Source: https://www.gov.uk/council-tax-bands
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-01-14T13:47:41+00:00

Find out the Council Tax band for a property, register any changes to the property or challenge the band in England or Wales by looking up the property's address or postcode online

Find out the Council Tax band for a home in England or Wales by looking up its address or postcode.

 For homes in Scotland, search on the Scottish Assessors website.

 You can also use this service to challenge your Council Tax band if you think it’s wrong. You’ll need to provide evidence for your challenge .

 This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

You can also challenge your Council Tax band by emailing:

 ctinbox@hmrc.gov.uk

 If you cannot use the online service, you can get extra support from the Valuation Office .
