# Renew your short-term medical driving licence

Source: https://www.gov.uk/renew-medical-driving-licence
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-02-10T09:25:06+00:00

Reapply for a short-term medical driving licence issued for 1, 2, 3 or 5 years because of a medical condition

You can renew your short-term medical driving licence if:

 you have one because of a medical condition

 it lasts for 1, 2, 3 or 5 years

 it’s about to expire, or already has

 This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 Renew online

 You’ll have one hour to add information once you start. You cannot save and return to your application later.

Find out about possible delays and who cannot use this service.

 Delays if your application is referred to a doctor

 Your application may take longer than usual if it needs to be referred to a doctor.

 You may be able to drive while DVLA is considering your application if both the following apply:

 you have a current driving licence

 your doctor or optician has not told you that you should not drive

 Read ‘Can I drive while my application is with DVLA ?’ for more guidance.

 Who cannot use this service

 You cannot use this service to:

 renew a full or provisional car or motorcycle licence - use the renew your driving licence service instead

 renew a full or provisional licence for a minibus, bus, coach or lorry - get forms D2 and D4 from a Post Office that offers DVLA services 

 change the name on your driving licence - contact DVLA instead

 renew a driving licence that was issued in Northern Ireland 

 Use a different service to reapply for a driving licence if yours was taken away or you surrendered it because of a medical condition.

 Renew by post

 You can also renew your short-term medical driving licence by post. DVLA will send you a renewal reminder 90 days before your licence expires.

 If you do not receive a reminder, you should get a D1 form from a Post Office and download and fill in the medical forms for your condition . Send them all to DVLA - the address is on the forms.
