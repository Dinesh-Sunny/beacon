# Request a repair to a council property

Source: https://www.gov.uk/repair-council-property
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:20+01:00

Request a repair to a council property to your local council - urgent problems, Right to Repair, health, safety, security

The council has to fix urgent problems in council owned homes which could affect the tenant’s health, safety or security within a certain period. This is known as the Right to Repair scheme.

Available in England and Wales only
