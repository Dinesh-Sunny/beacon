# Check how to register for Self Assessment

Source: https://www.gov.uk/register-for-self-assessment
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2020-11-18T15:31:17+00:00

How to register for Self Assessment or reactivate your account, if you need to send a tax return or want to make voluntary Class 2 National Insurance payments.

You must tell HM Revenue and Customs ( HMRC ) by 5 October 2026 if you need to complete a tax return for the previous tax year and you have either:

 not sent a tax return before

 registered before but did not need to send a tax return for the tax year 2024 to 2025

 The previous tax year started on 6 April 2025 and ended on 5 April 2026.

 You can tell HMRC by registering for Self Assessment.

 Find out how to register your client if you’re an agent .

 If you tell HMRC after 5 October 2026, you could get a penalty.

 This service is also available  in Welsh (Cymraeg) .
