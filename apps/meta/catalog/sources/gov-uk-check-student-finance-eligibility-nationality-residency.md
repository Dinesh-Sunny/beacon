# Check what student finance you can apply for depending on your nationality and residency

Source: https://www.gov.uk/check-student-finance-eligibility-nationality-residency
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-08-14T12:02:56+01:00

The student loans you can apply for based on your nationality and where you normally live.

Your eligibility for student finance is determined by your nationality and where you live.

 Use this tool to check if you can apply for:

 a Tuition Fee Loan and a Maintenance Loan (also known as ‘full support’)

 a Tuition Fee Loan only (also known as ‘tuition fee only funding’)

 A Tuition Fee Loan can help pay your course tuition fees. A Maintenance Loan can help with your living costs.

 Whether you qualify for student finance also depends on:

 your university, college or training provider

 your course

 how much previous study you’ve done

 your age

 The tool cannot tell you if your application will be successful, or about funding for most postgraduate study .

 Use this tool if you’re from England or somewhere outside the UK.

 There’s a different way to apply for student finance if you:

 live in Scotland  

 live in Wales 

 live in Northern Ireland  

 live in Jersey 

 live in Guernsey  

 live in the Isle of Man
