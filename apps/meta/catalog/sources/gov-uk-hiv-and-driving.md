# HIV and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/hiv-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T13:34:30+01:00

You do not need to tell DVLA if you have HIV

You do not need to tell DVLA if you have HIV or AIDS.

 You only need to tell DVLA if you develop a health condition that affects your driving .
