# Swap your council or housing association home

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-swap-homes-council
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:19+01:00

Exchanging your council or housing association property if you want to move house - tenants, rules, permission from landlords, mutual exchange

You can swap your council or housing association home with another tenant if you follow certain rules and get permission from your landlord. This is often called ‘mutual exchange’.

 Contact your landlord if you’re a housing association tenant and want to swap homes.

Available in England and Wales only
