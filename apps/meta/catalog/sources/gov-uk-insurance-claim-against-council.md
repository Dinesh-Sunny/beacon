# Make an insurance claim against your council

Source: https://www.gov.uk/insurance-claim-against-council
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:19+01:00

If you're a council tenant you may be able to make an insurance claim against the council for any damage they are responsible for

Council tenants may make insurance claims against the council for any damage that the council is responsible for, like damage caused by contractors working on behalf of the council.

Available in England and Wales only
