# Apply for Youth Opportunity Funding

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-youth-opportunity-funding
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:22+01:00

The Youth Opportunities Fund provides money for young people to use on activities and projects - apply to your local council

Contact your council about the the Youth Opportunities Fund (YOF) - money for young people to use on activities and projects that are of benefit to them and their local community.

Available in England and Wales only
