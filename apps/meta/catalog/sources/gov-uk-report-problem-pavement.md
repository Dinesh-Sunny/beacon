# Report a problem with a pavement

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-problem-pavement
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:17+01:00

Report damaged pavements to the local council - broken or missing slabs, removing weeds

Most councils are responsible for maintaining the majority of pavements including removing weeds, replacing broken or missing slabs.

Available in England and Wales only
