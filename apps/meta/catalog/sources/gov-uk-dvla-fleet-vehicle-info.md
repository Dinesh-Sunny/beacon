# Get information about your DVLA Fleet Scheme vehicles

Source: https://www.gov.uk/dvla-fleet-vehicle-info
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-03-27T12:45:53+00:00

Get information on your DVLA Fleet Scheme vehicles including when they need taxing and when MOTs are due for renewal, request a V5C document, companies must be registered with the DVLA Fleet Scheme

You can get information about your fleet including:

 which vehicles need taxing and when

 MOT renewal dates

 This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

You’ll need your DVLA Fleet Scheme username and password.

 Find out more about the scheme or contact DVLA to join.

 You must have at least 50 vehicles registered to one address to join.

If you’re not in the DVLA Fleet Scheme you can:

 Get vehicle information from DVLA 

 Check the MOT status of a vehicle
