# Tell DVSA about a change to a coach or bus

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-change-to-coach-bus
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-01-27T12:00:06+00:00

Notify DVSA if you've made modifications to your coach or bus or registered it as a public service vehicle.

Use this service to tell the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ) about a change you’ve made to your coach or bus. You can also use the service to tell DVSA that you’ve registered your coach or bus as a public service vehicle ( PSV ).

 You must tell DVSA if you’ve:

 made your coach or bus more accessible for disabled passengers, for example by installing ramps or wheelchair restraints

 made any changes to your coach or bus that has been issued with an original Certificate of Initial Fitness ( COIF ) or approval certificate

 made changes to the registration mark of your coach or bus

 Sign in or create an account

 You need to sign in or create an account to use this service.

Before you start, you need to download and fill in a test application form .

 Use the service to upload your completed form.
