# Visual field defects and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/visual-field-defects-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-05T11:14:44+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have a visual field defect - download the correct form to let them know

You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorbike licence

 You must tell DVLA if you have a visual field defect.

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form V1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form V1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
