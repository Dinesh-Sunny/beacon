# Find a vehicle scrapyard

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-vehicle-scrapyard
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-09-23T12:06:06+01:00

Find an authorised scrapyard where you can take your vehicle to be scrapped

Find a vehicle scrapyard, breaker’s yard or dismantler to scrap your vehicle in England.

These are sometimes called authorised treatment facilities ( ATF ), car breakers or end of life vehicle treatment facilities.

 Scrapyards in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland

 There are different lists of:

 registered ATFs in Scotland 

 end of life vehicle treatment facilities in Wales
