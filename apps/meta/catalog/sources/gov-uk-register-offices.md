# Find a register office

Source: https://www.gov.uk/register-offices
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-03-10T09:57:08+00:00

Find a register office (registry office) - register and find records of births, marriages, civil partnerships and deaths, and order certificates

You can use a register office to:

 register a birth, death, marriage or civil partnership

 find records of a birth, death, marriage or civil partnership (if it was registered at that register office)

 order copies of certificates

 After registering a death, you can tell government organisations about the death through the Tell Us Once service .

This service works in England and Wales only. Find a register office in Scotland and Northern Ireland .
