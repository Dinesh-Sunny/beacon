# Local library services

Source: https://www.gov.uk/local-library-services
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:15+01:00

Find your nearest library to access services such as book loans, CD rentals and internet access

Your local library provides many services such as free access to reading and learning materials, free internet access, study space, and community activities. Many areas have a home library service or have a mobile library service.

Available in England and Wales only
