# Severe memory problems and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/severe-memory-problems-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T14:13:02+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you suffer from severe memory problems - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if you have severe memory problems.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Ask your doctor if you’re not sure if your memory problems are severe.

 You must tell DVLA if your condition will affect your driving.
