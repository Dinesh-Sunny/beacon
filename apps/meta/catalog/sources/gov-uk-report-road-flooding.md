# Report road flooding

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-road-flooding
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-07-16T17:03:24+01:00

If you find a flooded road or street, report it to your local council

Tell your council about a flooded public road or blocked ditch. Enter your postcode to go to your council’s website for more information.

Available in England and Wales only
