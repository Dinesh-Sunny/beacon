Book a National Driver Offender Retraining Scheme course

If you’ve been invited to take a National Driver Offender Retraining Scheme course, you can search for courses online. You have to book directly with the course provider.

You can only take a National Driver Offender Retraining Scheme course if you’ve got a letter inviting you to take a course.

 You need your driving licence number to log in and see availability online.

 There are several different courses under the scheme. Check your letter to find out which course you can take.

 The prices shown are a guideline - the course provider sets the final price.

 Contact the provider to book and pay for a course.
