# Find out about pest control

Source: https://www.gov.uk/pest-control
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-11-09T15:49:44+00:00

Your local council may help you get rid of common pests in your property like mice, wasps and bedbugs with pest control services

Contact your council to find out if they provide pest control services.

Available in England and Wales only
