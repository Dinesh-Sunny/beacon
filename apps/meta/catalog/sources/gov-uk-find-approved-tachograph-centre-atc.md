# Find your local Approved Tachograph Centre (ATC)

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-approved-tachograph-centre-atc
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-11-04T16:49:51+00:00

Find an Approved Tachograph Centre (ATC) to get your tachograph read

You can find an Approved Tachograph Centre (ATC) to have a tachograph installed, calibrated or inspected.

You will have to find the right centre for the type of tachograph you have - digital or analogue.

 There’s a different list of centres in Northern Ireland.
