# Find out if there's a family hub in your local area

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-family-hub-local-area
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2023-12-21T10:12:10+00:00

Find out if there's a family hub in your local area. Family hubs are a single place to get support for children, young people and families. This can include baby groups and parent classes, counselling services and financial advice.


Family hubs are a single place to get support for children, young people and families. This can include baby groups and parent classes, counselling services and financial advice.

Available in certain parts of England only. If there are no family hubs in your local area you can find your local council and search for family support services or find learning activities for your child
