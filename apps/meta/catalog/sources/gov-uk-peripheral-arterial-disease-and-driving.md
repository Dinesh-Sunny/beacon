# Peripheral arterial disease and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/peripheral-arterial-disease-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T14:08:53+01:00

You may need to tell DVLA if you have peripheral arterial disease - download the correct form if you need to let them know

You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not need to tell DVLA if you have peripheral arterial disease.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if you have peripheral arterial disease. Fill in form VOCH1 and send it to the address on the form.
