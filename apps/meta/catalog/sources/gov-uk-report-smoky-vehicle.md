# Report a smoky lorry or bus

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-smoky-vehicle
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2014-11-18T10:08:47+00:00

Report a smoky lorry or bus to the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA)

Report an excessively smoky lorry or bus exhaust to the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ).

 Contact your local police to report other types of smoky vehicle.
