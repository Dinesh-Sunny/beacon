# Get traffic information to plan your journey

Source: https://www.gov.uk/traffic-information
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-04-01T01:01:04+01:00

Get up-to-date information about traffic problems and road conditions across the UK.

Use these online tools to get information about traffic and roadworks on motorways and A roads in England.

 Live traffic information

 Get up-to-date information about incidents that are currently causing delays and congestion on major roads managed by National Highways, for example accidents, broken down vehicles and emergency roadworks.

 Visit the National Highways website for live information about traffic and road conditions across the major road network.

 Traffic information for major roads in Scotland and Wales is available from:

 Traffic Scotland 

 Traffic Wales 

 Roadworks

 Search the database of current and planned roadworks to find out if your journey will be delayed by roadworks happening on the major road network.

 Road projects

 Get detailed information about longer term projects like planned maintenance and road improvement schemes.

 You can find out:

 how much the projects cost

 when they will start and finish

 how the work will affect journey times

 what effect the work will have on the environment

 Find National Highways road projects by region and search by road name.
