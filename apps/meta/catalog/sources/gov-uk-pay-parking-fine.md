# Pay a parking fine

Source: https://www.gov.uk/pay-parking-fine
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-20T15:01:05+01:00

Find out how to pay a local council for a parking fine or parking ticket - if it’s a penalty charge notice (PCN)

Pay a parking penalty charge notice ( PCN ) issued by a local council.

 Use the council’s postcode.

 If you get a text message from Department for Transport (DfT) telling you to pay an unpaid parking fine, this is a scam. DfT does not issue parking fines. Read advice on what to do if you receive a scam message .

Available in England and Wales only.

 If your parking fine is not from a council

 Check the parking ticket to find out who issued it and how to pay .
