# Severe communication disorders and driving 

Source: https://www.gov.uk/severe-communication-disorders-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2020-03-04T14:24:31+00:00

You must tell DVLA if you have a severe communication disorder and it affects your driving - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if your severe communication disorder affects your ability to drive safely.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Ask your doctor if you’re not sure if your severe communication disorder will affect your driving.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form A1 and send it to DVLA. The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form A1V and send it to DVLA. The address is on the form.
