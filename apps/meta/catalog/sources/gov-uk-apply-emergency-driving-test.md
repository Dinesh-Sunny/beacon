# Emergency driving tests for critical workers

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-emergency-driving-test
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2026-02-03T17:00:00+00:00

For NHS workers and the emergency services in England and Wales who need to drive and respond to ‘threats to life’. 

You can only get an emergency practical driving test if you’re a critical worker in England or Wales and you work for one of the following:

 the NHS, in health or social care

 the emergency services

 You must also:

 need to drive as part of your job

 respond to ‘threats to life’ as part of your job

 This service is not available in Scotland.

 How to get a test

 If you’re eligible and you want a test, talk to your employer. They will need to contact the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) .

 If you’re not eligible for an emergency test, you can still book a normal driving test .
