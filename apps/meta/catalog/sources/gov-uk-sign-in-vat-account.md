# Sign in to your VAT online account

Source: https://www.gov.uk/sign-in-vat-account
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-12-03T17:07:59+00:00

Sign in and submit ESL, RCSL and EU VAT refund application, and get help with lost username, password or account activation codes



Use this service to:

 find out when your VAT returns are due

 set up a Direct Debit to pay VAT 

 view your payments and how much you owe

 check and appeal penalties

 report changes to your business

 cancel your VAT registration

 You cannot use this service to submit a quarterly VAT return .

You’ll need to sign in to use this service. If you do not already have sign in details, you’ll be able to create them.

 You’ll also need your VAT number.

 You get these when you register for VAT .

 This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 Other VAT services

 You can also sign in to your VAT online account to:

 submit an EC Sales List ( ESL ) if you trade from Northern Ireland

 submit a Reverse Charge Sales List ( RCSL ) - find out how to do this by reading services received from overseas suppliers in paying VAT on imports from outside the UK to Great Britain and from outside the EU to Northern Ireland

 apply for EU VAT refunds if you trade from Northern Ireland

 tell HMRC you’ve brought a vehicle into the UK using the Notification of Vehicle Arrivals ( NOVA ) service

 make corrections to your VAT MOSS (Mini One Stop Shop) scheme registration information

 Service issues

 Online services may be slow during busy times. Check if there are any problems with this service .
