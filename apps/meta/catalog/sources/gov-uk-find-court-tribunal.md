Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-court-tribunal
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-09-13T11:29:36+01:00

Use court finder to find a court in England and Wales or a tribunal in England, Wales and Scotland

Find contact details and information on courts and tribunals in England and Wales, and some non-devolved tribunals in Scotland.

 You can find the following details:

 address

 contact details

 opening times

 how to get to the court or tribunal

 disabled access to the building

 This service is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .
