# Contact the Student Loans Company

Source: https://www.gov.uk/contact-student-loans-company
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-11-29T21:00:03+00:00

Contact details for the Student Loans Company – including repayment and refund enquiries.

Contact the Student Loans Company if you need help with things like:

 repaying your loan

 getting a refund

 making a complaint about your loan repayment

 This page is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 You can contact them in English or Welsh.

 If you want to speak to an adviser about someone else’s account, you’ll need to get their consent before contacting the Student Loans Company .

 If you’re still studying and need help with your student finance, you should contact:

 Student Finance England 

 Student Awards Agency Scotland 

 Student Finance Wales 

 Student Finance Northern Ireland
