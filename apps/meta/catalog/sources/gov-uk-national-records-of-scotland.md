Contact information for the National Records of Scotland 

The National Records of Scotland store records of births, deaths, marriages, civil partnerships, divorces, stillbirths.

 They’re also responsible for the Scottish national archives, which contain government documents and public records.

 You can contact the National Records of Scotland online or by telephone .

 You can also write to the following address.

National Records of Scotland General Register House 2 Princes Street Edinburgh Scotland EH1 3YY
