# Find an electric vehicle (EV) chargepoint installer

Source: https://www.gov.uk/electric-vehicle-chargepoint-installers
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2022-12-14T13:22:05+00:00

Find an approved installer to check how much it costs to install an electric vehicle chargepoint socket. Check if you’re eligible to apply for funding support.


Contact an installer approved by the Office for Zero Emission Vehicles (OZEV) to find out how much it costs to install an electric vehicle chargepoint socket.

 You can apply for a grant to help with these costs if you:

 own a flat or rent any residential property 

 are a landlord 

 have workplace parking 

 are a state-funded education institution 

 are a household with on-street parking
