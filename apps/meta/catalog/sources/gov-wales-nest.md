 Living in a cold home?

 Nest provides free advice and home improvements to help you save energy and money.

 We know the rising cost of living is difficult, especially if you’re struggling to heat your home. In winter, it’s more important than ever to make sure your home is safe and warm.
 The Welsh Government Warm Homes Nest scheme is here to help you.
 Since 2011, Nest has provided over 60,000 households with free home energy improvements. These improvements have included heating systems, insulation and solar panels to help lower energy bills and improve health and wellbeing.
 When you contact us, our friendly advisors will offer free impartial advice to help you lower your energy bills and improve your health and wellbeing with:
 the best energy and water tariff for your home
 benefits to boost your household income
 help to lower your carbon footprint
 advice on installing your own low carbon technology
 Our advisors can also help you find out if you can get free home energy efficiency improvements. 
 If you are eligible, we can recommend a tailored package of improvements. This could include a boiler repair or replacement, heat pump, insulation and solar panels.
 If you are not eligible, we can help you find other schemes, that suit your circumstances.
