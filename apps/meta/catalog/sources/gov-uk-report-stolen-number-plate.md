# What to do if your number plate is stolen

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-stolen-number-plate
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2026-10-07T09:31:08+01:00

What to do when your vehicle number plate has been stolen: reporting it to the police and telling DVLA.

This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 There are two steps to reporting stolen number plates.

 Report the stolen number plates to the police to get a crime reference number.

 Tell DVLA and include the crime reference number.

 There is a different way to report if your number plate has been cloned .

 Reporting stolen number plates to the police

 Contact the police , who will give you a crime reference number. You’ll need to include this number when you tell DVLA.

 Tell DVLA about the stolen number plates

 Send a letter to DVLA with:

 your vehicle registration number (from the stolen number plates)

 the crime reference number from the police

 You must be the registered keeper of the vehicle or have permission from them.

 Send your letter to:

DVLA Swansea SA99 1AR

 After you tell DVLA

 DVLA will send a confirmation letter to the address on your vehicle log book (V5C).

 Getting new number plates

 You’ll need to get new number plates for your vehicle before you can drive it. How to get new number plates depends on the type that was stolen.

 Standard number plates

 If you had standard number plates, you can get number plates made up .
