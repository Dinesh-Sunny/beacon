# Report discarded syringes

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-syringes
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:20+01:00

Report discarded syringes, needles and other drug related items in public places to your council

Your council will arrange for the removal and disposal of discarded syringes and other drug related items in public places.

Available in England and Wales only
