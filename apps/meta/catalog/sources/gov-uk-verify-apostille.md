# Verify an Apostille

Source: https://www.gov.uk/verify-apostille
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-12-16T16:59:40+00:00

Check the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office created an Apostille - verify a legalised document

Use this service if you want to verify either a paper or electronic Apostille certificate issued in the UK.

You’ll need:

 the date the Apostille was issued

 the Apostille number

 The issue date and number are on the Apostille certificate.
