# Student finance forms for courses starting before 1 January 2027

Source: https://www.gov.uk/student-finance-forms
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-02-12T13:10:03+00:00

Download Student Finance England application forms and guidance notes - including forms PN1, PR1 and DSA1.

You can use this form finder to get the forms you need for student finance if all of the following are true:

 you’re studying in the UK

 you normally live in England

 your undergraduate course starts before 1 January 2027

 This can include support such as:

 a Tuition Fee Loan to help pay your course tuition fees

 a Maintenance Loan to help with your living costs

 If your course starts on or after 1 January 2027 and you’re getting Lifelong Learning Entitlement ( LLE ) funding, do not use this form finder. Use your online account or contact Student Finance England ( SFE ) .

 Use the form finder to download forms and guidance notes, including:

 PN1, PR1 and PTMN for students who qualify for a Tuition Fee Loan and a Maintenance Loan (full support)

 EU26N for full-time students who qualify for a Tuition Fee Loan only (tuition fee only funding)

 EUPTL1 for part-time students who qualify for a Tuition Fee Loan only (tuition fee only funding) 

 DSA1 for Disabled Students’ Allowance ( DSA )

 There’s a different way to download forms if you’re supporting your child or partner’s student finance application .

 Before you start

 Check if you’re eligible for student finance and the type of funding you could get.

 The forms are different for students from Scotland , students from Wales and students from Northern Ireland .
