# Track your driving licence application

Source: https://www.gov.uk/track-your-driving-licence-application
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2014-12-04T00:01:11+00:00

Track your driving licence application if you've already made an online application

If you’ve applied for a new driving licence or renewed your driving licence online, you can track its progress.

 This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 Use your driver and vehicles account to track your application.

 Sign in or create an account

 You’ll need to sign in to your driver and vehicles account to use this service. If you do not already have a driver and vehicles account, you’ll be able to create one.

 You’ll be told when you sign in if you need to prove your identity. This is to keep your details safe and usually involves using photo ID like a passport or driving licence.

 You’ll also need your application reference. This was emailed to you after you applied. This will be a series of 12 letters, for example TYAB-ABCD-ABCD.

 Start now 

 How long applications usually take

 You’ll normally get your driving licence within a week of completing your online application.

 If you applied by post

 Contact DVLA to track your application.
