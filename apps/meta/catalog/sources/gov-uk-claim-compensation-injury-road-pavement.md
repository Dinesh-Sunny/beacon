# Claim compensation for injuries caused by roads or pavements

Source: https://www.gov.uk/claim-compensation-injury-road-pavement
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:21+01:00

You may be able to claim compensation from the council if you've had an accident caused by a road or pavement

If you’ve been injured because of a hazard on a road or pavement, you may be able to claim compensation from the local council. Enter the postcode to go to the council’s website for more information.

Available in England and Wales only
