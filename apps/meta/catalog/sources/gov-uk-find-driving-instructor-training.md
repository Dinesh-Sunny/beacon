# Find driving instructor training courses

Source: https://www.gov.uk/find-driving-instructor-training
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2019-07-29T15:18:58+01:00

Find training courses to qualify to become an approved driving instructor (ADI)

Find training courses to:

 qualify to become an approved driving instructor ( ADI )

 prepare for your ADI standards check 

 improve your skills as part of your continuing professional development ( CPD )

The trainers are on the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency’s official register of driving instructor training - which is sometimes called ORDIT .

 There’s a different process to become an ADI in Northern Ireland.
