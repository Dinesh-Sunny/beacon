# Check if a heat pump could be suitable for you

Source: https://www.gov.uk/check-heat-pump
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2022-07-04T17:10:03+01:00

Check if a heat pump is a suitable option for heating your home, and the type of heat pump system you may be able to install.

Find out if a heat pump could be a suitable choice of central heating system before you install one.

 You’ll need to give the following information about your home:

 whether it’s in England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland

 what type of heating system it has (for example, gas or electric)

 how many bedrooms it has

 This service will compare the cost and carbon emission savings available to you and what you’ll need to install a heat pump.
