# Get, view or stop MOT reminders

Source: https://www.gov.uk/get-stop-view-mot-reminder
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-10-18T14:29:59+01:00

Sign up to get free text or email reminders before your MOT is due. View or cancel existing MOT reminders.

Use this service to:

 get free MOT reminders by text message or email

 view your existing reminders

 stop getting reminders

 You cannot use this service if you live in Northern Ireland . You’ll get a reminder by post 7 weeks before your MOT is due.

 Get MOT reminders

 You’ll get a reminder: 

 one month before your car, van or motorcycle MOT is due

 2 months before your lorry, bus or large trailer MOT is due

 To sign up, you’ll need:

 the vehicle’s number plate (registration number)

 a mobile number or email address

 Sign up 

 You can be fined up to £1,000 for driving a vehicle without a valid MOT.

 View or stop your MOT reminders

 You can check when your next reminder is due or unsubscribe to stop getting reminders.

 You’ll need:

 the vehicle’s number plate (registration number)

 the email address or mobile number you signed up with

 View or stop your MOT reminders .

 When to stop your reminders

 You have to unsubscribe to stop getting reminders for a vehicle you’ve:

 sold

 transferred to someone else

 scrapped or written-off

 registered as off the road

 Other ways to stop MOT reminders

 You can also unsubscribe by:

 following the instructions in the text message you received 

 clicking the unsubscribe link in the email you’ve been sent

 You’ll pay the cost of a standard text message to unsubscribe from text reminders.

 Check if a vehicle has a safety recall

 Your reminder might tell you if your vehicle has been recalled for a safety reason. This depends on the manufacturer. You can also check if a vehicle model, part or accessory has been recalled .

 Get help with reminders

 Contact the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ) to get help with MOT reminders.
