## Get information about your State Pension

Contact the Pension Service to get information about:

 your State Pension

 Pension Credit 

 the over 80 pension 

 missing Cold Weather Payments and Christmas Bonus payments 

 getting extra payments from a deceased husband, wife or civil partner’s State Pension

 making voluntary National Insurance contributions if you’ve already reached State Pension age - if you’ve not reached State Pension age, call the Future Pensions Centre instead

 becoming an appointee for someone claiming State Pension

 If you live in Northern Ireland, contact the Northern Ireland Pension Centre .

 If you live abroad, contact the International Pension Centre .

 If you want information about someone else’s State Pension, use the ‘alternative enquiry’ service .

 This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

## Claim your State Pension

You can claim your State Pension online , by phone or by post.

 If you reached State Pension age before 6 April 2016, you need to claim the basic State Pension instead. If you’re over 80, you might also be able to make a claim for the over 80 pension .

 If you want to claim on someone else’s behalf, you must be able to manage their claim .

 Claim by phone or post

 You can claim by phone if you reach State Pension age in the next 4 months.

 If you want to claim by post, phone the Pension Service to get a State Pension claim form sent to you.

 Contact the International Pension Centre if you live abroad.

## Report changes of address, bank details or other circumstances

If you get State Pension or the over 80 pension, you must report if you:

 moved to a new address in the UK (only tell them after you’ve moved)

 moved or intend to move to another country permanently

 moved into a residential care home, even if only temporarily (only tell them after you’ve moved in)

 start or stop getting a benefit from the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP)

 change your bank details

 marry or form a civil partnership

 get divorced or your civil partnership is dissolved

 are widowed or your civil partner dies

 go to prison

 are held in legal custody

 If you claim Pension Credit, there are extra changes that you need to report .

 If you live abroad, contact the International Pension Centre instead.

 If you’re reporting a change on someone else’s behalf, you can use the ‘alternative enquiry’ service .
