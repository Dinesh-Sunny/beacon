# Report a problem with a street light

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-problem-street-light
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2016-08-31T15:08:02+01:00

Report a street light problem to the local council - they are responsible for installation and maintenance of street lighting

Your council is responsible for the installation and maintenance of street lights.

Major roads in England

 Contact Highways England if the street light is on a motorway or an ‘A road’ they manage in England .
