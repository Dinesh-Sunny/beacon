# Severe depression and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/severe-depression-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2022-06-22T12:16:00+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have severe depression: how to report it to DVLA, what form to download and send depending on the vehicle you drive. 


You must tell DVLA if you have severe depression.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Find out what you need to do if you have psychotic depression .

 Car or motorbike licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form M1 and send it to DVLA to report your condition. The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form M1V and send it to DVLA to report your condition. The address is on the form.
