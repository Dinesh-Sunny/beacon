# Register for alerts about careers in international organisations

Source: https://www.gov.uk/register-jobs-international-organisations
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-12-11T13:39:13+00:00

Register your details to receive alerts about career opportunities in international organisations (like the United Nations or European institutions).

If you’re a British national, you can get email alerts for job vacancies in international organisations including:

 the United Nations

 the European External Action Service (EEAS)

 the Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe (OSCE)

 humanitarian organisations

 The vacancies can cover a range of a different fields, such as:

 humanitarian and development work

 climate and the environment

 science, technology and artificial intelligence

 Your email alerts will be for job vacancies that match your preferences. You could also be contacted directly if you’re suitable for a priority job vacancy.

 The service is run by Impactpool. You’ll need to create an account with them when you register.
