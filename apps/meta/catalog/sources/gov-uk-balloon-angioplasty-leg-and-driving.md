# Balloon angioplasty (leg) and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/balloon-angioplasty-leg-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-01T12:47:16+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have a balloon angioplasty in your leg and drive a bus, coach or lorry - download the correct form to let them know


You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not need to tell DVLA if you’ve had a balloon angioplasty in your leg.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if you’ve had a balloon angioplasty in your leg.

 Fill in form VOCH1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
