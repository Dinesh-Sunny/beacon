# Leukaemia and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/leukaemia-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T13:48:57+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have leukaemia and a bus, coach or lorry licence - download the correct form if you need to let them know

You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not need to tell DVLA if you have leukaemia.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if you have leukaemia.

 Fill in form C1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
