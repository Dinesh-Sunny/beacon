# Transient global amnesia and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/transient-global-amnesia-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-11-13T15:23:58+00:00

You may need to tell DVLA if you have transient global amnesia - download the correct form to let them know

You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not have to tell DVLA .

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if you’ve had 2 or more episodes of transient global amnesia. Fill in form B1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
