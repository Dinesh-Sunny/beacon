# Kidney problems and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/kidney-problems-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T13:52:30+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have kidney problems and drive a bus, coach or lorry - download the correct form to let them know


You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not need to tell DVLA if you have kidney problems.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if you have kidney problems.

 Fill in form K1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
