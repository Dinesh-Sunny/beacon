# Report an obstruction on the road

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-obstruction-road
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-04-01T01:01:02+01:00

You can report obstructions on the road such as illegal signs, hedges, builders skips and scaffolding to the local council or National Highways.

Report obstructions on the road, such as mud, illegal signs, overhanging tree branches, hedges, builders skips or scaffolding, to the local council or National Highways.

 Local roads

 Contact the local council to report obstructions on local roads.

 You can also tell your council about blockages of public rights of way .

Major roads in England

 Contact National Highways if the obstruction is on a motorway or an A road they manage in England .
