Get help and advice from your local council if you're homeless or about to lose your home.

Get help and advice from the local council if you’re homeless or about to lose your home.

 There’s a different way to get help in Scotland and to get help in Northern Ireland .

 Enter a postcode where you have a local connection . This might be where you’ve lived recently, have close family or work.

If you’re applying for housing support from a council in England

 You do not need to have a local connection if you are:

 a current or former member of the UK armed forces

 a bereaved spouse or civil partner losing Ministry of Defence housing due to a service-related death

 a current or former Reservist with a service-attributed serious injury or illness

 a care leaver who is under 25

 a survivor of domestic abuse
