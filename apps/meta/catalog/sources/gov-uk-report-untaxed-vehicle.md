# Report an untaxed vehicle

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-untaxed-vehicle
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2017-05-05T12:27:38+01:00

Anonymously report an untaxed vehicle that you’ve seen.

If you see an untaxed vehicle on a road, you can report it. Your report is anonymous and will be investigated.

 There’s a different way to report:

 a driving or insurance offence 

 an abandoned vehicle 

 a vehicle with no MOT 

 This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 Steps to report a vehicle as untaxed

 Check if the vehicle is taxed before you report it - this is a free service.

 Make a note of the vehicle registration number, make, model and colour of the vehicle and the street name, town and the postcode where you saw it. You will not be able to report the vehicle without these details.

 Report the vehicle online .
