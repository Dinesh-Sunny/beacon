# Apply for equipment for your home if you're disabled

Source: https://www.gov.uk/apply-home-equipment-for-disabled
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-05-02T17:27:22+01:00

Apply to your local council for equipment or help adapting your home if you’re disabled

If you need to adapt your home because of a disability or old age, you can apply to the council for equipment or help.

Available in England and Wales only
