 How we can help

 We provide a free and impartial service that aims to resolve disputes between consumers and suppliers in the energy sector. 

 If you're having issues with your energy supplier, energy broker, network operator, green deal or heat network supplier, we may be able to help resolve your dispute.

 How we can help 

 Our process

 Our simple process makes it easier than ever to raise a dispute with your energy supplier:

 Step 1

 Raise the issue with your energy supplier, allowing them time to resolve it* 

 Step 2

 Register your dispute with us via our website, telephone, email or post* 

 Step 3

 Review any early resolution offers sent from the supplier (FCR)** 

 Step 4

 If no offers are accepted, both parties must provide evidence within 14 days. 

 Step 5

 We ll then review the dispute and notify both parties when we reach a decision 

 Step 6

 If you accept the decision, the supplier has 28 days to implement the remedy 

 Our process 
 *Before raising your dispute with us, please allow your provider 8 weeks to put things right (unless you receive a deadlock letter from them sooner).

 **In some cases, suppliers may acknowledge fault and try to resolve your dispute with an offer before we start our investigation. This is called a Facilitated Complaint Resolution (FCR) .

 What to expect
 What are the possible outcomes of raising your dispute through our service?
