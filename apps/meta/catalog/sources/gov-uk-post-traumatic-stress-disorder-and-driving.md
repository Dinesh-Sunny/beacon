# Post traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/post-traumatic-stress-disorder-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-12-23T09:35:27+00:00

You must tell DVLA if you have post-traumatic stress disorder and it affects your driving - download the correct form to let them know

You must tell DVLA if you’re diagnosed with post traumatic stress disorder ( PTSD ) and it affects your ability to drive safely.

 If your driving is not affected by PTSD , you do not need to tell DVLA . Ask your doctor if you’re unsure.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about PTSD that affects your ability to drive safely. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form M1 and send it to DVLA if you need to tell them about your condition. The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form M1V and send it to DVLA if you need to tell them about your condition. The address is on the form.
