# Parents' Learning Allowance

Source: https://www.gov.uk/parents-learning-allowance
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-02-03T10:15:37+00:00

## Overview

If you’re a student with children, you may be able to apply for help with your learning costs. This is called Parents’ Learning Allowance.

 You could be eligible if you’re either:

 in full-time higher education

 studying a course worth at least 120 credits in the year you’re applying for, funded through the Lifelong Learning Entitlement (LLE) 

 How much you get depends on your household income.

 The allowance:

 does not have to be paid back

 is paid on top of your other student finance

 will not affect your benefits or tax credit

 You may have to pay back any money you are overpaid because of a change in your circumstances or course.

 You cannot get a Parents’ Learning Allowance if you’re getting a Postgraduate Loan.

## What you'll get

Depending on your household income, you could get between £50 and £2,024 for each year of your course.

 The money is paid directly into your bank account in up to 3 instalments per year, depending on the length of your course.

 Parents’ Learning Allowance is paid on top of your other student finance and does not have to be paid back.

## Eligibility

You’re eligible for Parents’ Learning Allowance if all of the following apply:

 you’re a student in England

 you have dependent children

 your household income is less than £18,957.99 a year

 In addition, you must be studying one of the following:

 a full-time undergraduate course

 a course that’s worth at least 120 credits for the year you’re applying

 You do not need to be paying for childcare to be eligible.

## How to apply

You can apply for the Parents’ Learning Allowance when you apply for student finance .

 After you apply

 Your student finance account will be updated to show how much Parents’ Learning Allowance you’ll get. You’ll get an email when this is ready to view.
