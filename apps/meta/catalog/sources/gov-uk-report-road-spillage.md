# Report a road spillage

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-road-spillage
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-04-01T01:01:04+01:00

Report an incident of spillage on the road such as oil, mud, sand or dropped cargo to the local council

Report spillages on the road, such as oil, mud, sand or dropped cargo, to the local council or Highways England.

 Local roads

 Contact the local council to report spillages on local roads.

Major roads

 Contact Highways England if the spillage is on a motorway or an A road they manage in England .
