# Psychotic depression and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/psychotic-depression-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-07T14:06:44+01:00

You must tell DVLA if you have psychotic depression - download the correct form to let them know 

You must tell DVLA if you have psychotic depression.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. You may be prosecuted if you’re involved in an accident as a result.

 Find out what you need to do if you have severe depression .

 Car or motorbike licence

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form M1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 Fill in form M1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
