# Report a blocked drain

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-blocked-drain
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2012-07-16T17:03:42+01:00

Your local council is responsible for public drains - report a blockage.

Tell your council about a blocked public drain. Enter your postcode to go to your council’s website for more information.

Available in England and Wales only
