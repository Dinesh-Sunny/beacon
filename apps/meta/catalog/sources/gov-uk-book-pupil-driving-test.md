# Book and manage driving and riding tests for your pupils

Source: https://www.gov.uk/book-pupil-driving-test
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2024-11-08T12:39:47+00:00

Book driving and motorcycle tests for your pupils, check details, change, swap or cancel tests, and manage your availability for car driving tests.

If you’re an instructor or trainer, use this service to:

 book motorcycle and other types of driving tests for your pupils

 change, swap or cancel other types of appointments

 confirm your pupil’s details before the test

 You cannot change, swap or cancel car driving tests for your pupils.

 Learners must book their own appointment for car driving tests.

 There’s a different way to manage your availability as a driving instructor .

 It’s against the law to book a car driving test for someone else.

 Book and manage tests online

 You need your:

 Government Gateway user ID and password

 credit or debit card, or pre-funded account 

 pupil’s driving licence number when you book a car test, or confirm their details for other types of tests

 You must have approval to book tests before you make a booking.

 This service is available from 6am to midnight.

You can book these types of driving tests:

 motorcycle (module 1 and module 2)

 lorry and bus, including the Driver Certificate of Professional Competence ( CPC ) part 3a, part 3b and part 4 tests

 approved driving instructor ( ADI ) part 2 (driving ability) and ADI part 3 (instructional ability) tests

 You can only book tests for learner drivers or riders who are being taught by you or your business.

 If you’re a motorcycle trainer

 You can also use this service to book motorcycle manoeuvring areas to practise on.

 Book without a pupil’s details

 You can book some tests up to 10 weeks in advance without giving your pupil’s details. This is known as ‘trainer booking’.

 You can only do this for these types of tests:

 motorcycle (module 1 and module 2)

 lorry and bus, including the Driver CPC tests

 You must confirm the pupil’s details by 9pm at least one clear working day before the test. Working days do not include Sundays and public holidays.

 Example

 Confirm the pupil’s details by 9pm on Wednesday if the test is on Friday.

 Change or cancel a test

 You must give notice to change or cancel a test, or you’ll have to pay again.

 To get a refund, you must give at least 3 full working days’ notice for all tests you book using this service.

 Monday to Saturday count as working days but Sundays and public holidays do not.

 Find out when you can get a refund if you change a driving test or cancel a driving test at short notice.

 If you regularly cancel trainer booking tests

 You may not be allowed to book motorcycle, bus or lorry trainer booking tests if you cancel either:

 25% or more of your tests

 20% or more of your tests within 10 clear working days of the test

 Help with your bookings

 Contact the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency ( DVSA ) to get help booking driving tests for your pupils.
