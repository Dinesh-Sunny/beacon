# Febrile convulsions and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/febrile-convulsions-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2022-12-06T14:18:21+00:00

Febrile convulsions is a medical condition you must tell DVLA about

You must tell DVLA if you have febrile convulsions.

 You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. If you’re involved in an accident, you may be prosecuted.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You must tell DVLA .

 Report your condition online 

 You can also fill in form FEP1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA .

 Fill in form FEP1V and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
