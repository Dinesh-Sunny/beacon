# Nystagmus and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/nystagmus-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-06T14:16:28+01:00

You do not need to tell DVLA if you have nystagmus, as long as you meet the standards of vision for driving

You do not need to tell DVLA if you have nystagmus, as long as you meet the standards of vision for driving .

 You may still need to tell DVLA about other medical conditions .
