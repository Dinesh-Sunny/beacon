# Carer's Allowance: report changes

Source: https://www.gov.uk/carers-allowance-report-change
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-03-06T12:01:02+00:00

Report changes in circumstances if you're claiming or have applied for Carer's Allowance - if you get a job, take a break from caring for someone, stop being a carer

You must report changes in circumstances if you’re claiming or have applied for Carer’s Allowance, for example if:

 you change, start or leave your job

 you start earning more than £204 a week

 you or the person you care for change address

 you stop being a carer

 you stop providing at least 35 hours of care a week

 you take a holiday or go into hospital - even if you arrange care while you’re away

 the person you care for goes into hospital, into a care home or takes a holiday

 You cannot use this service to report the death of someone you’re caring for. You should use the Tell Us Once service instead.

 There’s a different way to report a change if you live in Scotland and get Carer Support Payment .

 This guide is also available in Welsh (Cymraeg) .

 Report changes online

 Make sure you have:

 your National Insurance number

 details of the person you’re caring for

 details of your changes

 There’s a different way to report changes in Northern Ireland .

This will take around 10 minutes.

 Other ways to apply

 You can also report a change of circumstances to the Carer’s Allowance Unit by phone or by post.
