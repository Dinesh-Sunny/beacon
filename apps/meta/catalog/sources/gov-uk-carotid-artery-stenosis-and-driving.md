# Carotid artery stenosis and driving

Source: https://www.gov.uk/carotid-artery-stenosis-and-driving
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2015-10-06T14:27:48+01:00

If you have carotid artery stenosis you may need to tell DVLA - download the correct form to let them know

You can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not tell DVLA about a medical condition that affects your driving. If you’re involved in an accident, you may be prosecuted.

 Car or motorcycle licence

 You do not need to tell DVLA if you have carotid artery stenosis.

 Bus, coach or lorry licence

 You must tell DVLA if you have carotid artery stenosis.

 Fill in form VOCH1 and send it to DVLA . The address is on the form.
