# Report a mistake on your MOT record

Use this form to report a mistake with mileage, vehicle details, the country where the vehicle was registered or the name or location of the MOT centre.

Use this online form to report a mistake on your MOT record.

 You cannot use the form if your MOT was in Northern Ireland. You’ll need to contact the Driver and Vehicle Agency (DVA) instead.

 You can use the form to report a mistake with:

 mileage, if the MOT was more than 28 days ago

 the colour, make, model or fuel type of the vehicle

 the country where the vehicle was registered

 the name or location of the MOT centre

 You can also use the form to find out how to report a mistake with:

 mileage, if the MOT was in the last 28 days

 the MOT due date

 the MOT history (a missing test or a test that should not be there)

 You cannot change or remove MOT results, defects or any ‘advisory’ problems. You can appeal the result of your MOT if you think it’s wrong.

 Before you start

 To report a mistake using the form, you’ll need to give:

 your name

 your driving licence number

 your address

 your phone number

 your email address

 the vehicle details, such as the make and model

 the vehicle’s registration number (number plate)

 the vehicle’s identification number (VIN)

 the MOT details, such as the MOT test date and number, if you’re correcting the mileage or MOT centre details

 If you want to correct the vehicle mileage

 You’ll need to upload one of the following documents to show the correct mileage at the time of the MOT:

 an invoice

 an emissions printout

 a service receipt

 the vehicle’s service history

 a vehicle job card or inspection sheet from the MOT centre

 You cannot use an MOT certificate or a photo of the dashboard display showing the mileage.

 The document must show:

 the vehicle registration number (number plate) or vehicle identification number (VIN)

 the mileage

 a date within one day of the MOT test

 Report a mistake online

 You’ll usually get a reply within 8 weeks, but it may take longer.
