# Report a dead or injured animal

Source: https://www.gov.uk/report-dead-animal
Fetched: 2026-11-04
Publisher updated: 2025-01-27T11:38:57+00:00

Report a dead or injured animal such as a badger, fox or domestic pet found on the road

What you need to do depends on the type of animal, and whether you were involved in an accident with it.

 You must stop and contact the police as soon as you can if you hit any dogs, horses, donkeys, mules, pigs, goats, sheep or cattle (such as cows) with your vehicle. It’s a criminal offence if you injure the animal and do not report the accident.

 If you find a dead animal

 You can report most dead animals to the local council.

 You can report a dead wild bird online .

 There’s a different way to contact your local council in Scotland or contact your local council in Northern Ireland .

If you find an injured animal

 Report an injured pet or wild animal

 You can report injured pets and wild animals to the RSPCA if you’re in England or Wales, or the equivalent organisations if you’re in Scotland or Northern Ireland.

 Report a concern about a farm animal’s welfare

 You might want to report a concern about a farm animal’s welfare if it:

 has signs of neglect, like starvation

 appears to have been abused or mistreated

 has strayed, is stranded or is stuck

 If you’re in England, Scotland or Wales, you can report a welfare concern about a farm animal to the Animal and Plant Health Agency ( APHA ).
